CoRoute community guidelines

CoRoute works because riders trust each other: with their location, their phone, and sometimes their safety. These guidelines apply to everything you share in CoRoute: ride chat, group talk, usernames, profile pictures, crews and polls, trip and place reviews, and photos. They add to the Terms of Use.

The basics

  1. Safety first. Never use CoRoute in a way that puts you or others at risk. Ride within the law and your abilities. Read and tap only when it is safe; CoRoute holds most buttons until you stop.
  2. Respect everyone. No harassment, threats, bullying, hate speech, or insults about caste, religion, gender, region, language or anything else.
  3. No stalking or tracking. Never use CoRoute to follow or track someone without their knowledge and consent. Do not share another rider's location, phone number or details outside the ride.
  4. Be honest. No fake accounts, no pretending to be someone else, no pretending to be CoRoute staff.
  5. Keep it legal. No illegal content, no promotion of racing on public roads, stunts in traffic, or riding drunk.

SOS and safety features

  • Use SOS for real emergencies. Testing? Tell your group first, then close it with False alarm.
  • Never send false SOS alerts on purpose. Accounts that raise many false alarms get fewer requests sent to other groups, and deliberate misuse can lead to suspension. Your own group is always alerted.
  • When you tap I Can Help, go. If you can no longer help, say so.
  • Medical info and emergency contacts you see while helping are for that emergency only. Never copy, share or keep them.

Ride chat and group talk

  • Keep it about the ride. Quick replies exist so nobody has to type while moving.
  • No spam, adverts or chain messages.
  • Group talk is live and not recorded, but the same rules apply.
  • Report a message: long-press it and choose Report message (it is also hidden for you). Or choose Hide messages from that rider. The reported rider is not told who reported them.

Usernames and profile pictures

  • No offensive words, no impersonation, no phone numbers or e-mail addresses in your username.
  • Profile pictures: you, your bike, or something that represents you. No nudity, violence, hate symbols or other people's photos without permission.

Friends and crews

  • Send friend requests to people you know or ride with. Repeated unwanted requests count as harassment.
  • Block anyone who bothers you (People › the rider › Block). They are not told and cannot send you requests or see your profile.
  • Crew admins keep their crew friendly: remove members who break these rules and keep polls about riding.
  • Polls: no polls designed to mock or target a member.

Reviews and photos

  • Review places and rides honestly, from your own visit.
  • No fake reviews, no reviews in return for payment or favours, no reviews of your own business.
  • No personal attacks on staff or owners; describe the experience.
  • Photos: only photos you took, of the place or your group, with everyone's agreement. No faces of strangers in close-up, no number plates of other vehicles, no children without a parent's agreement.
  • Every photo and every review chosen for the website is checked by the CoRoute team first. Photos appear only after approval. You can withdraw a review at any time from the trip report.

Public rides and nearby groups

  • Public rides are visible to riders on the same road. Treat other groups with respect; a wave is a wave, not an invitation to follow.
  • Report a public ride or rider that behaves badly from the ride's report option.

How to report

What How
A chat message Long-press › Report message
A rider bothering you Block them, then Profile › Help › Send feedback › Report a problem with their username
A crew, poll, review or photo Send feedback › Report a problem, with the crew name or place
Something dangerous happening now Call 112. Then report in CoRoute.

Reports are read by the CoRoute team. We may ask you for details by e-mail if you allowed replies.

What happens when rules are broken

Depending on how serious it is and whether it has happened before:

  1. Content removed: a message, review, photo, picture, poll or crew.
  2. Warning: a message from CoRoute appears on your Home screen.
  3. Feature limits: for example fewer help requests to other groups after repeated false alarms, or suspension from public rides.
  4. Account suspension: for serious or repeated breaches.
  5. Report to the police where the law requires it or someone is in danger.

Safety features keep working for every rider while they have an account. If you think we made a mistake, reply through Send feedback and we will look again.
