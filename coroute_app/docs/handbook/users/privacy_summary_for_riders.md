# Your privacy on CoRoute

This is a short, plain summary. The full privacy policy is on the CoRoute website (in the app: **Profile › Help › Privacy & terms › Privacy policy**). If anything here differs from the full policy, the full policy applies.

**In one paragraph.** CoRoute collects what a safe group ride needs, shares your position only with the group you ride with and only during the ride, and never sells your data or shows ads. There are no third-party trackers or analytics in the app. Your data is stored in India.

## What we collect and why

| What | Why | Kept for |
|---|---|---|
| **Account:** name, username, e-mail, phone number, password (stored only as a hash) or your Google sign-in | To sign you in and let your group reach you | Until you delete your account |
| **Emergency contact** | To alert them if you crash or send an SOS | Until you change it or delete your account |
| **Medical info** (optional): blood group, allergies, notes | So the people helping you know what matters | Until you clear it or delete your account |
| **Bike details:** type, colour, model, engine, tank, mileage | Your bike marker on the map, fuel range | Until you delete them |
| **Live location**, speed and battery level | The group map, fall-behind alerts and SOS | During the ride; recorded routes deleted after **90 days** |
| **Trips:** reports, timeline, chat and quick replies in the ride | Your trip history and the group's shared timeline | Until you delete them; exact positions removed after 90 days |
| **Five ride counts** for your rider score (hard brakes, breaks, highest tiredness, break reminders, minutes over the limit) | Your private rider score | With your account |
| **Voice** | Live group talk | **Never stored.** Only who talked and when, for 7 days, to stop abuse |
| **Reviews and photos** you choose to send | Trip and place reviews | Until you delete them |
| **Diagnostics log** (only if you send it) | To fix problems such as a late alert | 30 days on our server |

**Stays on your phone:** crash detection and motion data, siren listening, your tank and mileage numbers, the diagnostics log (until you send it), saved maps (deleted 7 days after the trip) and the safety summary on your trip report.

**Your profile picture:** the round picture is what signed-in riders see. A smaller copy of the picture you chose is kept so you can adjust it later; **only you can open it**. Location and camera details in the file are never kept. Removing the picture deletes both.

**Private places** (Settings › Road safety): up to 3 places kept on your phone only. Within 300 m of them your route is not recorded or uploaded and nothing goes into the anonymous road data. Your live position during a ride is not hidden (group, guardian link, SOS).

## Who sees what

| Who | What they see |
|---|---|
| **Your group, during a ride** | Your name, username, bike, phone number, live position, messages. Your emergency contact. Your medical info **only while your SOS or crash alert is open**. |
| **Friends** | Your username, picture, first name and ride totals |
| **Other riders** | Your username and picture only |
| **Guardians** you share a link with | What you chose: Basic (status), Live (your location) or Emergency only. Medical info only during an open alert. |
| **A rider of another group who accepted to help you** | Your first name, bike type and colour; while your alert is open, your medical info and emergency contact. **Never your phone number.** |
| **Public leaderboards** | Your username and rounded totals, unless you hide yourself |
| **CoRoute staff** | What they need for safety and support (for example open SOS alerts). They **cannot** see medical info or listen to voice. Every change they make to your account and every view of a diagnostics log is recorded. |

## Help from nearby riders: always on

When you send an SOS or crash alert, your own group is always alerted first. CoRoute may also ask up to 3 riders of other groups who are riding toward you on the same road. This is part of CoRoute's safety features and has no switch, because it can bring help sooner.

- **Before anyone accepts,** they see only the emergency point, how far it is and roughly how long it takes. No name, no group, no phone number.
- **After a rider accepts,** they see your first name and your bike type and colour, and, only while your alert is open, your medical info and emergency contact.
- **Never shared:** your phone number and your ride mates' numbers.
- When the alert closes, they lose access at once.
- In the same way, you may be asked to help a rider nearby. You can always choose **Can't Assist**.

## Microphone and sirens

The microphone is used only during a ride: for group talk when you talk, and for **Siren alerts**. Siren listening runs on your phone; the sound is analysed in short pieces and thrown away. Nothing is recorded or sent. You can turn Siren alerts off in Settings.

## Emergency texts and live links

- In an SOS your emergency contact gets a text with your name, the time, a live link and your coordinates. The live link shows your first name and position for 30 minutes, or until the alert closes.
- The text is sent by your phone, from your own number through your carrier (normal SMS charges apply), only if you allowed emergency texts. CoRoute cannot read your messages. When the SOS closes, the same people get a "safe now" text.
- CoRoute's servers do not send these texts. An SMS provider would be used only if CoRoute turns one on (off today).

## Diagnostics: your choice

The app keeps a technical log on your phone (7 days, 20 MB at most) to help fix problems. It never contains messages, names, phone numbers, e-mail addresses, medical info, passwords or addresses; locations are rounded to about 100 m and written only during rides. It leaves your phone only when you send it: **Send feedback › Attach diagnostics log**, the "send the log of this ride?" question after a safety event, or a support request you approve. **Send safety logs automatically** is off unless you turn it on.

## Help make roads safer: your choice (off by default)

Road safety warnings (hazard ahead, rough road, twisty roads, no-fuel gaps) reach every rider. Sharing your own
anonymous road and safety signals is **off until you turn it on** (Settings > Road safety, or /account). When on:
signals as areas of about 150 m (never your route), not the first and last 300 m of a ride, a monthly one-way code
instead of your id, shown only once 5 riders passed a spot, raw signals deleted after 90 days. Download or delete
your contributions at any time; older-than-90-days totals are anonymous and cannot be split out.

## Your choices

- **Switches you control:** Siren alerts, voice alerts, push notifications (each kind), leaderboards, data saver, battery saver.
- **Always on, for your safety:** crash detection, SOS, emergency texts, Medical ID for responders, help nearby riders, ask nearby riders to help me.
- **Guardian links:** stop any link at any time.
- **Delete trips** from your trip history.
- **Your phone during a ride:** to recognise your own phone when you sign in again during a ride (for example after a reinstall), the app sends a one-way hash of an app-specific device ID and a random installation ID. Our server keeps only a further keyed hash, uses it for nothing else and deletes it with the session. While you are on a ride, other devices can only sign in with a one-time code we e-mail you.
- **App lock (optional):** your fingerprint or screen lock is checked by your phone; nothing about it is sent to us.
- **Delete your account:** **Profile › Account › Delete account**. It is removed at once: your profile, trips, routes, contacts, safety log entries, chat messages and logs. Rides you shared stay in other riders' history with your name replaced by "Former rider". If you cannot sign in, use the account deletion page on the CoRoute website.
- **Ask for a copy** of your data, or a correction, through the contact address in the privacy policy.

## Children

CoRoute is for licensed riders aged 18 and over.
