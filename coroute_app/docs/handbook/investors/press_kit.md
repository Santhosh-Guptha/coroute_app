### 200 words

CoRoute is a safety and coordination app for motorcycle riders in India, built for group rides and solo rides. Two-wheeler riders were 44.8 % of India's 1,72,890 road deaths in 2023 (Ministry of Road Transport and Highways), yet most group rides are still organised on WhatsApp, which has no safety layer.

CoRoute turns the rider's own phone into a crash detector, an SOS beacon and a group radio, with no extra hardware. After a hard stop the app asks "Possible crash. Are you OK?" with a 30-second countdown; if the rider does not answer, the group and emergency contacts are alerted with the location. The SOS still gets through without mobile data: the phone texts contacts and nearby riders, the server texts emergency contacts, and phones relay the alert to each other. For riders wearing noise-cancelling earbuds or a helmet set, CoRoute listens for ambulance, police and fire-engine sirens and says "Keep left and give way".

Groups share one live map with lead, middle and sweeper roles, fall-behind alerts and regroup points, and talk hands-free over a live intercom with wind noise reduced. Family can follow a ride through a browser link. CoRoute has no ads and no trackers, and every safety feature is free.

## Feature highlights

1. **Crash detection with a 30-second check.** "Possible crash. Are you OK?" Tap "I'm OK" to cancel or "Need help" to send at once; no answer sends the SOS. Designed to ignore potholes, speed breakers and rough roads.
2. **SOS that works without signal.** Hold for 3 seconds. Without internet, the phone texts the emergency contact, lead and nearest riders a live map link; the server also texts emergency contacts once the alert reaches it; nearby phones relay the alert.
3. **Siren alerts for riders who can't hear outside.** The phone's microphone recognises ambulance, police and fire-engine sirens and speaks a give-way alert in the earbuds or helmet set, even with noise cancelling on.
4. **Live hands-free intercom.** Hands-free or push-to-talk, to everyone or to one rider, with wind and breath noise cleaned up. Voice is never recorded.
5. **Guardian live link.** Family follow the ride in a browser, no app needed; first name and position only; the link ends on its own.
6. **One live group map.** Lead, middle and sweeper roles; the sweeper is told when a rider falls behind; regroup points; group speed limits.
7. **Medical ID.** Blood group, allergies and notes on the lock screen and shared with responders only while an SOS is open.
8. **Trip reports.** Distance, riding time, stops, a shared ride map and replay; a private rider score that rewards smooth riding, never speed.

## Approved claims and wording

- Say: "safety app", "helps riders get help", "alerts your group and emergency contacts".
- Do not say: "certified safety device", "saves lives", "guarantees help", "works everywhere". CoRoute's terms describe it as an aid, not a safety device; in an emergency riders should contact local emergency services directly.
- Siren alerts: one phone microphone cannot tell direction, so never write that CoRoute knows a siren is "behind" you.
