# CoRoute for Tour Operators, Clubs and Fleets

*One console for every rider on every ride you run, with the same safety features each rider already gets in the app.*

Version: CoRoute 3.56 · November 2026 · For: tour operators, riding clubs, rental fleets, corporate fleets

---

## In one paragraph

CoRoute is a group riding app for motorcyclists, built for Indian roads and with safety as the first priority. Every rider gets crash detection, SOS, emergency texts and a live group map. The **Operator console** is a web console for organisations that run many rides for many riders. You keep a **roster** of your riders and schedule **rides**. You watch **every live ride on one map**, with SOS alerts at the top, and you see **attendance** after each ride. You can send short **broadcasts** to the roster and publish **public sign-up pages** for your rides. You apply on the CoRoute website. The CoRoute team reviews each application and sets your limits when it approves you.

## Who it is for

| You are a | Typical use |
|---|---|
| **Tour operator** | Multi-day tours with a lead, a sweeper and a support crew. You need to know where every customer is and to act fast when something goes wrong. |
| **Riding club** | Weekend and monthly club rides, sometimes several groups on the same day. You need a roster, sign-ups and a way to tell members about the next ride. |
| **Rental fleet** | Customers on your bikes, often riding roads they do not know. You need to see who is riding now and be told at once if someone is in trouble. |
| **Corporate fleet** | Staff who ride for work, or company group rides. You need attendance records and a safety net for every rider. |

## What you get

### 1. Safety for every rider, on every ride
The safety features live in each rider's CoRoute app. They work on operator rides exactly as on any CoRoute ride, and no plan or access setting ever turns them off.

- **Crash detection** on the rider's phone, with a 30-second countdown and "I'm OK" / "Need help". If nobody answers, the SOS goes out by itself.
- **"Bike down?" check** when the bike falls while stopped or at walking pace and nothing moves for a minute.
- **SOS** with a 1.5-second hold. It reaches the group, the lead and the sweeper, and the rider's emergency contact by text. The text carries a live position link.
- **Group safety**:
  - a "missing rider" check when someone stops while the group rides on;
  - a group spread alert for the lead;
  - a sweeper alert when a rider drops behind the sweeper;
  - buddy pairs for groups of 7 or more;
  - suggested regroup points and break suggestions.
- **On the SOS screen**:
  - the nearest hospital with a Navigate button;
  - one tap to dial 112;
  - sharing a live link with anyone.
- **Medical info** on the rider's lock screen. It goes to the people helping only while an SOS or crash alert is open.

### 2. One live map for all your rides
- Every live ride appears on one map. Each ride has its own colour, the lead and sweeper are marked **L** and **S**, and a rider whose phone has had no signal for 5 minutes is shown faded. The map refreshes every 5 seconds.
- An SOS or crash alert puts a **red banner** at the top and an **SOS** badge in the navigation, whichever page you are on.
- **Emergency contact during an SOS.** While a rider has an open SOS in your ride, you can open their emergency contact, their phone number, their medical info and their last position. You see none of this at any other time, and every view is recorded.

### 3. Roster and attendance
- Invite riders by CoRoute username, or share an invite link. Riders accept in the app or on the website, and they can leave at any time.
- After each ride you see who joined, who left and when, who finished, the distance from the stored tracks and the incidents (type, time and outcome). Monthly totals are included.
- Export a CSV for your records, if the export feature is in your plan. It contains no locations, phone numbers or e-mail addresses.

### 4. Rides that become normal CoRoute group rides
- Schedule a ride with a name, start time (India time), meeting point, destination, notes, number of riders, a **lead** and a **sweeper**.
- On the day, **Start** turns it into a CoRoute group ride led by your lead rider. **Invite roster** sends ride invites that riders accept in the app.
- The seats come from your operator limits, so your riders do not need a plan of their own.

### 5. Broadcasts and public sign-ups
- **Broadcasts**: a short title and message to everyone on the roster, shown in the app as a "Community update". You can send up to 5 a day.
- **Public sign-up page** for a ride on the CoRoute website, with or without your approval of each sign-up. Once the ride is live, approved riders see the join code on that page.

## Privacy, in plain words
- **You see**: usernames, first names and riding roles, and join times. During your own live rides you also see each rider's position, speed and battery.
- **You never see** a rider's phone, e-mail or medical info. The one exception is the emergency contact view, available only while that rider has an open SOS in your ride. Every view is recorded in an audit log.
- Reports and CSV files contain **no coordinates**.
- Riders can leave your club at any time, from the website or the app.

## How pricing and limits work
- **Approval.** Every operator is reviewed by the CoRoute team before the console opens.
- **Limits are set by CoRoute when it approves you.** They start from a template for your operator type and can be changed later:

| Limit | Tour operator | Riding club | Rental fleet | Corporate fleet |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Riders on the roster (waiting invites count) | 200 | 150 | 100 | 300 |
| Live rides at the same time | 3 | 2 | 5 | 5 |
| Rides per month (India time) | 30 | 12 | 60 | 40 |
| Riders per ride (lead included) | 30 | 40 | 12 | 25 |
| Co-organisers | 3 | 3 | 2 | 5 |
| Validity | 365 days | 365 days | 365 days | 365 days |

These are starting defaults, not promises. CoRoute may set different numbers for your account, and the server also applies an overall ceiling on riders per ride.

- **Features** are switched on per operator:
  - the multi-group live map;
  - emergency contacts during an SOS;
  - attendance;
  - the ride calendar and public sign-ups;
  - broadcasts;
  - CSV export.

  By default, public sign-ups are off for rental and corporate fleets, and CSV export is off for riding clubs.

## What is not included yet (Planned)
These items are on the CoRoute plan but **not built** in 3.56:
- staff roles beyond owner and co-organiser, such as a viewer role;
- a fleet garage (assigning your bikes to riders for each ride);
- per-bike billing and GST invoices from the console;
- fuel-stop plans;
- paid ride registration (collecting fees);
- a consent screen in the app at join ("This ride is run by ...").

## How to apply
1. Install CoRoute and create your account (the one you ride with).
2. Go to **/operators/apply** on the CoRoute website and sign in.
3. Fill in:
   - your type;
   - the organisation name and city;
   - the expected number of riders;
   - your business e-mail and phone (only the CoRoute team sees them);
   - an optional website or social page;
   - a short description of the rides you run.
4. Press **Send application**. Your status shows as *Waiting for review*. The page says "We review applications within a few working days and reply to your business e-mail."
5. Once approved, sign in at **/operator** with the same account.
