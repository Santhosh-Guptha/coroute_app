### If something goes wrong
1. **Crash:** your phone counts down 30 s. Tap **I'm OK** if you are fine; if you do not answer, the SOS goes out by itself.
2. **Need help:** **hold SOS for 1.5 s.** The group, the lead, the sweeper and your emergency contact are alerted, with a live link.
3. **Someone hurt:** make the scene safe and **CALL 112** (ambulance 108). Do not move the rider unless there is danger. Do not remove the helmet unless trained.
4. **Siren:** keep left and give way. Never stop in the carriageway or on a blind bend.
5. **Left behind or stopped:** answer "Are you OK?" on your phone, or tap **Wait for me**.

<div class="privacy">During an open SOS, the organiser can see your emergency contact, your phone and your medical info, to help you. This view is recorded. At other times they see only your username, first name and, during the ride, your position.</div>
