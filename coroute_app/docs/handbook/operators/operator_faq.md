# Operator FAQ

*Common questions from tour operators, clubs and fleets.*

Version: CoRoute 3.56 · November 2026

---

## Getting started

**Who can apply?**
Tour operators, riding clubs, rental fleets and corporate fleets. You apply at **/operators/apply** with your CoRoute account. The CoRoute team reviews each application.

**How long does approval take?**
The application page says "We review applications within a few working days and reply to your business e-mail." [OWNER TO FILL: target time, if you want to commit to one.]

**What does it cost?**
[OWNER TO FILL.] Your limits and features are set by CoRoute when you are approved. Riders do not pay to join your rides.

**My application was rejected. Can I apply again?**
Yes. The reason is shown on /operators/apply and at /operator. Fix the issue and send it again.

**Do I need a separate login?**
No. You and your co-organisers sign in at **/operator** with your normal CoRoute accounts.

## Riders and roster

**Do riders need to be my friends in CoRoute?**
No. Invite them by username. They still have to accept the invite.

**Where do riders see my invite?**
In the app under **People > Requests** (it appears as a crew invite), and on the website at **/clubs**.

**Can I add riders without their consent?**
No. Every rider accepts the invite themselves, or joins with your link.

**Why does my roster say "full" when I have fewer members than the limit?**
Waiting invites count toward the limit. Cancel old invites under **Waiting for an answer**. Invites expire after 14 days anyway.

**Can riders leave?**
Yes, at any time, from /clubs or by leaving the crew in the app. Only the owner cannot leave their own club.

**Do my riders need a CoRoute plan?**
No. Operator rides take their seats from your operator limits. Joining a ride is free.

## Rides

**What happens when I press Start?**
A normal CoRoute group ride starts, with your lead rider as trip owner. It appears on the lead's phone. Then press **Invite roster** so riders get the ride invite. Public sign-ups who were approved see the join code on the ride page.

**Can I run more than one ride at the same time?**
Yes, up to your "Live rides at once" limit. With the multi-group feature, all of them appear on one map.

**My group is bigger than my riders-per-ride limit.**
Split it into two rides (for example A and B groups), each with its own lead and sweeper. You can also ask CoRoute about your limit.

**Do cancelled rides count against my monthly limit?**
No.

**Who controls the route and the group speed limit?**
The lead, in the app (**Group settings**). The console sets the lead and sweeper and can change riding roles during the ride.

**Can I edit a ride after it starts?**
No. Only a scheduled ride can be edited. During a live ride you can change roles, invite the roster or end the ride.

## Safety and SOS

**What do I see when a rider raises an SOS?**
A red banner on the console and an **SOS** badge in the navigation. If your plan includes "Roster & emergency contacts during an SOS", you can open the rider's emergency contact, their phone, their medical info and their last position, while the SOS is open.

**Does CoRoute call the ambulance or police?**
No. **Call 112** in any life-threatening emergency. CoRoute alerts the group, the lead and sweeper, the rider's emergency contact (by text, with a live link) and, where the rider needs help, nearby CoRoute riders. The SOS screen in the app has one-tap 112 and the nearest hospital.

**What if the rider has no signal?**
The SOS is kept on the phone and sent when there is a connection. Nearby CoRoute phones can relay it, and the phone can text the emergency contact through its messages app.

**Can crash detection miss a crash or raise a false alarm?**
Yes. It depends on the phone, its mount and its sensors. The rider has 30 seconds to tap "I'm OK" on a false alarm. CoRoute learns from false alarms on each bike, but that learning never raises the threshold for severe impacts. Brief riders to keep the phone in a handlebar mount.

**Why is a rider shown faded on the map?**
Their phone has not sent a position for 5 minutes. On mountain roads this is often only coverage, but check with the sweeper.

**Does a suspension or expiry stop a live ride?**
No. Live rides keep running with all safety features. Only console actions stop.

## Privacy

**Can I see riders' phone numbers or e-mail?**
No. The one exception is the emergency contact view during that rider's open SOS, and each view is recorded.

**Can I see where riders live, or their past routes?**
No. You see positions only during your own live rides. Reports and CSV files have no coordinates.

**What is in the CSV export?**
For each rider and ride: ride, ride date, status, username, first name, role, joined, left, finished, distance and incidents. Nothing else.

**Who can see my business phone and e-mail?**
Only you and CoRoute administrators.

## Broadcasts and partners

**Why was my broadcast refused?**
Broadcasts cannot contain prices, offers, coupons or buying words (Google Play rules for notifications), phone numbers or e-mail addresses. You can send up to 5 a day.

**Can I promote a sponsor or partner deal to my roster?**
Not through broadcasts. Partner perks are curated and sent by CoRoute, only to riders who turned **Partner perks** on. See *Partner Program*.

## Account

**Can I transfer my operator account to someone else?**
Not in the console. Contact CoRoute.

**Can I have staff with view-only access?**
Not yet. Co-organisers have full ride access. A viewer role is **Planned**.

**Can I assign my rental bikes to riders, or invoice from the console?**
Not yet. Fleet garage, per-bike billing and GST invoices are **Planned**.

**What happens if I delete my CoRoute account?**
Your operator account, its rides, broadcasts and club roster are removed. Your riders remain CoRoute users.
