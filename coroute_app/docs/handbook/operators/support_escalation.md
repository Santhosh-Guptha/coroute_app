# Support and Escalation

*How operators reach CoRoute, what to expect, and what to do in an emergency.*

Version: CoRoute 3.56 · November 2026

---

## Rule number one

> **In any emergency involving injury or danger to life, call 112 first.** For an ambulance you can also call 108. CoRoute does not dispatch emergency services and does not monitor your rides. Contact CoRoute only after the emergency services have been called.

## 3. During a live ride: escalation path

1. **Someone hurt or in danger?** → **Call 112.** The lead or sweeper stays with the rider (see *Ride Organiser SOP*, section 4).
2. **The console does not show the ride or the SOS**:
   - the riders' app still works without the console. SOS, crash alerts, emergency texts and group alerts run on the riders' phones and CoRoute's servers;
   - ask the lead to check the ride in the app (riders' list, incident card);
   - reload the console. If you were signed out, sign in again;
   - if the problem continues, contact CoRoute at S1 (section 1).
3. **A rider's emergency contact is not shown**:
   - check that your plan has "Roster & emergency contacts during an SOS";
   - the rider may not have saved a contact ("No emergency contact saved.");
   - the riders on scene can use the app's incident card and the rider's lock-screen medical info.
4. **After the emergency**: write down the times and actions. If you need CoRoute's records, for example the alert time or the audit of contact views, request them at S3 with the ride name and time.

## 4. Account problems

| Situation | What you see | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Limits reached | e.g. "Your plan allows 12 rides a month; 2026-11 is full." | Ask CoRoute for a change (S3). See the error table in the *Operator Console Manual*. |
| Feature missing | "<feature> is not part of your plan." | Ask CoRoute to add it (S3). |
| Account expired | Banner "This operator account expired on <date>…" | Ask CoRoute to extend it (S3). Reports stay readable. |
| Account suspended | "Account suspended" with the reason | Contact CoRoute (S3). Live rides keep running. |
| Console turned off | "The operator console is turned off for now." | This is temporary. Live rides are not affected. Check CoRoute's notices or contact CoRoute. |

## 5. Rider complaints and data requests

- When a rider asks you about their data, refer requests about CoRoute's data to CoRoute support. Handle requests about your own copies (for example exported CSV files or incident logs) yourself. See *Data Processing Note (DRAFT)*.
- A rider can always leave your club on /clubs or in the app.
- Report suspected misuse of rider data, or a breach, to CoRoute **without undue delay** [OWNER TO FILL: e.g. within 24 hours].

## 6. What CoRoute may ask you
- The CoRoute usernames involved, the ride name and times.
- Whether 112 or the police were called, and when (for incident reviews).
- Confirmation that you have deleted exported data when it is no longer needed.
