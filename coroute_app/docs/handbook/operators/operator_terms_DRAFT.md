# CoRoute Operator Terms (DRAFT)

> **DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW. Not legal advice and not an offer.** This draft sets out the commercial and operational points that CoRoute's lawyers should turn into binding terms. Square brackets mark items to complete or confirm. Where it describes how the product works, it reflects CoRoute 3.56 as built.

**Parties:** [CoRoute legal entity name, address] ("CoRoute") and the organisation approved to use the Operator console ("Operator").

**Effective date:** the date CoRoute approves the Operator's application.

## 1. Definitions
- **Console**: the CoRoute Operator web console at /operator and the related website pages (/clubs, /rides/<slug>).
- **Owner**: the CoRoute account that applied for and holds the Operator account.
- **Co-organiser**: a roster member to whom the Owner has given console access.
- **Roster**: the riders who have accepted the Operator's invitation (the "club").
- **Operator ride**: a ride scheduled in the Console and started as a CoRoute group ride.
- **SOS data**: a rider's emergency contact (name and phone), phone number, medical information and last position, shown in the Console only during an open SOS or crash alert in an Operator ride.
- **Limits and features**: the values set by CoRoute on approval (roster size, live rides at once, rides per month, riders per ride, co-organisers, validity, and the feature switches).

## 2. Application, approval and term
2.1 The Operator applies on the CoRoute website. CoRoute may approve, reject (with a reason) or ask for more information, at its discretion.

2.2 On approval, CoRoute sets the limits, features and validity period. CoRoute may change them by notice [OWNER TO FILL: notice period, except for urgent safety, security or legal reasons].

2.3 The term runs until the "valid until" date shown in the Console, unless it is renewed or terminated earlier. After expiry, the Operator can read its reports but cannot make changes.

## 4. Operator responsibilities
The Operator shall:
- (a) run its rides lawfully, holding any permits, licences, registrations and insurance its activity requires (for example tour operator, rental or commercial vehicle rules);
- (b) brief riders on safety before each ride, assign a competent lead and, where reasonable, a sweeper, and follow its own safety procedures (see the CoRoute *Ride Organiser SOP* as guidance);
- (c) obtain any rider agreements, waivers and consents it needs (see the *Rider Consent and Waiver (DRAFT)* template);
- (d) keep account credentials secure, give console access only to trusted Co-organisers, and remove access promptly when a person leaves;
- (e) use the Console only to organise rides and keep riders safe;
- (f) not invite people who have not agreed to join, and respect riders' decisions to decline or leave;
- (g) not use broadcasts, ride notes or announcements for prices, offers, buying messages, phone numbers or e-mail addresses (the Console enforces this), or for unlawful, misleading, hateful or unsafe content;
- (h) ensure that public ride pages describe the ride accurately (time, meeting point, requirements).

## 5. Safety position
5.1 CoRoute provides software tools, including crash detection, SOS, group alerts and live location. They depend on riders' phones, batteries, sensors, mobile networks and GPS, and **may fail, be delayed or raise false alarms**.

5.2 CoRoute does not dispatch emergency services and does not monitor rides. The Operator remains responsible for its own safety arrangements, and must **call 112** (or direct others to) in an emergency.

5.3 Live rides keep running, with all safety features, through a suspension or expiry of the Operator account.

## 7. Acceptable use and suspension
7.1 CoRoute may suspend the Console, with a reason shown to the Operator, for:

- a safety, security or legal concern;
- misuse of rider data;

- non-payment [OWNER TO FILL].

7.2 Suspension stops all console actions. Rides already under way keep running.

7.3 CoRoute may reinstate the Operator after the issue is resolved.
