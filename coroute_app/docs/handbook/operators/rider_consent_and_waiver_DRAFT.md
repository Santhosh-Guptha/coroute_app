## 1. The ride
1.1 I am joining a motorcycle ride organised by the Organiser. I understand that motorcycling involves inherent risks, including falls, collisions, road and weather hazards, mechanical failure, wildlife, altitude and remoteness from medical help, and that these can lead to serious injury or death.

1.2 I confirm that I hold a valid driving licence for the vehicle I ride, that the vehicle is registered, insured and roadworthy (or provided by the Organiser under a separate agreement), and that I will wear a certified helmet and suitable protective gear.

1.3 I will follow traffic law, the Organiser's briefing and the instructions of the ride lead and sweeper. I will not ride under the influence of alcohol or drugs.

## 2. Use of the CoRoute app on this ride
2.1 The Organiser uses the CoRoute app and the CoRoute Operator console to organise the ride. My use of the app is also governed by CoRoute's own Terms and Privacy Policy, which I accepted when I created my account.

2.2 **What the Organiser can see.** By joining the Organiser's club or ride on CoRoute, I understand that the Organiser (its owner and co-organisers) can see:
- (a) my CoRoute username and first name, my role on the ride and when I joined or left;
- (b) **during the Organiser's live rides only**, my position, speed and phone battery level;
- (c) after the ride, attendance information: joined or left times, whether I finished, distance and incident type, time and outcome. Reports do not include map coordinates.

2.3 **Data shared during an SOS only.** If I raise an SOS, or my phone raises a crash alert, during the Organiser's ride, and **only while that alert is open**, the Organiser can view my emergency contact's name and phone number, my phone number, the medical information I saved in the app (such as blood group, allergies and notes) and my last known position. The purpose is to help me in an emergency. CoRoute records each such view in an audit log. The Organiser cannot see this information at any other time through CoRoute.

2.4 I understand that the same safety information may also reach my ride group, my guardian (if I set one) and riders who accept to help me during an open alert. ## 3. Limits of technology
3.1 I understand that CoRoute's safety features (crash detection, "Bike down?" checks, SOS, emergency texts, group alerts and live location) **depend on my phone, its battery, its sensors, mobile signal and GPS**. They may fail to detect an incident, may raise false alarms, and may be delayed or fail to deliver. They are aids, **not a substitute** for calling emergency services, for safe riding or for the Organiser's own safety arrangements.

3.2 In an emergency, the first step is to **call 112**. Neither CoRoute nor the Organiser guarantees that emergency services will be contacted automatically.
