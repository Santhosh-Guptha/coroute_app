# Ride Organiser SOP: Group Ride

*Standard operating procedure for operators, ride leads and sweepers running a group ride on CoRoute.*

Version: CoRoute 3.56 · November 2026 · Owner: [OWNER TO FILL: operator's safety lead]

> This SOP describes how to use CoRoute's built features on a group ride. It does not replace road law, your own risk assessment, first-aid training or the emergency services. **In a life-threatening emergency, call 112 first.**

---

## 1. Roles

| Role | Who | Responsibilities |
|---|---|---|
| **Operator / base** | Owner or co-organiser, at a laptop with the console | Starts the ride, invites the roster, watches the live map, opens the emergency contact during an SOS, and coordinates with family or authorities. |
| **Lead (L)** | Experienced rider on your roster. Trip owner in the app. | Sets the route and group settings, sets the pace, picks regroup and break points, ends the ride. Gets the spread and incident alerts. |
| **Sweeper (S)** | Experienced rider at the back | Rides at the back and never overtakes the last rider. Gets the "behind the sweeper" and "no reply" alerts. Stops with any rider who stops. |
| **Pack** | All other riders | Keep their place, use quick replies, follow the briefing. |

Optional, for larger groups: **buddy pairs**. CoRoute pairs riders automatically once a ride reaches 7 riders. The lead can reassign pairs in **Group settings > Buddy pairs**.

## 2. Pre-ride briefing checklist

Do this at the meeting point, 20 to 30 minutes before the start. A printable one-page version is in *safety_briefing_template.md*.

### 2.1 Riders and bikes
- [ ] Licence, registration, insurance and PUC papers carried (as your policy requires).
- [ ] Bike check (T-CLOCS style):
  - tyres (pressure, tread, cuts);
  - brakes front and rear;
  - chain slack and lube;
  - lights, indicators and horn;
  - mirrors;
  - fuel level against the first fuel stop;
  - no fluid leaks.
- [ ] Riding gear: certified helmet (strap fastened), gloves, jacket, boots, rain gear if forecast.
- [ ] Phone in a **handlebar mount**, charged or on a charger. CoRoute warns once per ride if the phone seems to be in a pocket or loose, because crash detection works best when mounted.

### 2.2 In the CoRoute app (every rider)
- [ ] Signed in, and has **accepted the ride invite** (or joined with the code).
- [ ] **Emergency contact** set in the profile.
- [ ] **Medical info** filled in: blood group, allergies, notes for a doctor. It shows on the lock screen and goes to the people helping only during an open SOS or crash alert.
- [ ] **Pre-ride phone check** run. It tests GPS, sensors, SOS readiness and battery in under a minute.
- [ ] Location, notifications, motion and microphone permissions allowed. The microphone is used for the intercom and siren alerts.
- [ ] Optional: a **guardian**, a family member who follows the ride live and gets "Arrived safely".
- [ ] Bike details filled in, or "I'm riding pillion".

### 2.3 Roles and plan
- [ ] Introduce the lead and the sweeper by name, and check that the app shows them (**L** and **S** on the map).
- [ ] Route, planned stops, **regroup points** and fuel stops explained.
- [ ] Hand signals and the overtaking rule agreed (no overtaking the lead, none inside the group on ghats).
- [ ] Riding formation (staggered) and the distance between riders agreed.

### 2.4 Group settings (lead's phone, Group settings)
- [ ] **Group speed limit**: Off, 40, 60, 80, 100 or 120 km/h. Riding over it for 10 seconds is logged and the group is told once.
- [ ] **Group spread alert (km)**: 1, 2, 3 or 5 (default 3). The lead hears "Group spread 4 kilometers, slow down" when the group stretches too far.
- [ ] Buddy pairs checked (7+ riders).

### 2.5 Communication
- [ ] **Intercom**: hold to talk to the whole group or to the leads. Test it once at the meeting point.
- [ ] **Ride chat quick actions**: one-tap tiles **Fuel, Break, Wait for me, I'm OK, Slow down, Regroup here, On my way, Arrived**. Typing works only when stopped. While moving, only the quick replies work.
- [ ] Agree that nobody handles the phone while moving. Cards that need a tap show their buttons only when stopped.

### 2.6 Emergency briefing (say it out loud)
- [ ] "If you crash, your phone counts down 30 seconds. Tap **I'm OK** if you are fine. If you do not answer, the SOS goes out by itself."
- [ ] "To raise an SOS yourself, **hold the SOS button for 1.5 seconds**."
- [ ] "The SOS reaches the group, the lead and sweeper, and your emergency contact by text with a live link."
- [ ] "If someone is hurt: make the scene safe, **call 112**, do not move the injured rider unless there is danger, and do not remove a helmet unless trained."
- [ ] Location of the first-aid kit, and who has first-aid training.

## 3. During the ride

### 3.1 Normal running
- The **lead** holds the agreed pace and watches for the **group spread** card. When it shows:
  - slow down;
  - or use **Set regroup point**. CoRoute suggests a fuel, food or rest place 2 to 10 km ahead of the last rider.
- The **sweeper** stays at the back. CoRoute alerts the sweeper and the lead when a rider drops behind the sweeper.
- **Missing-rider check**:
  - If a rider has been stopped for about 3 minutes while the group rides on, their phone asks "Are you OK? The group is moving on".
  - The rider can answer **I'm OK**, **Need help**, **Waiting for fuel**, **Photo stop** or **Taking a break**, or hold **Send SOS**.
  - If there is no answer in 90 seconds, the lead and sweeper get "No reply from <name>".
- **Roll call**: the ride sheet shows "Joined / Not yet" at the start and "Arrived / On the way" at the destination.
- **Base** keeps the live map open. The card of each ride shows riders, speed and last update.

### 3.2 Fuel and breaks
- Riders tap **Fuel** or **Break** in the chat quick actions so that the group knows why they stopped.
- **Break reminders**:
  - every rider gets a break reminder after 2 hours, then every hour;
  - it may come earlier if their phone sees signs of fatigue (time of day, wobble, micro-stops).
- **Group break planner**: when several riders are getting tired, the lead sees "Several riders need a break. Plan a stop?" with a suggested place. It never names who is tired.
- Plan fuel against the **smallest tank** in the group, not the lead's.

### 3.3 Sirens and road hazards
- **Emergency vehicle siren**:
  - CoRoute listens with the phone's own microphone during rides and says "Emergency vehicle siren close by. Keep left and give way.";
  - music pauses for the alert;
  - **Everyone keeps left and gives way.** The lead does not stop the group in the carriageway.
- **Road hazards**:
  - potholes and speed breakers felt by the rider at the front warn the riders behind;
  - stretches where many riders brake hard trigger "Many riders brake hard on this stretch. Ride carefully.";
  - the pack slows down and keeps its spacing.
- **Low battery**: below 10% CoRoute switches to an SOS battery saver, and the group is told if a phone shuts down. The lead should plan a charging stop.

## 4. Emergency procedure: SOS or crash alarm

### 4.1 What raises an alert
- a rider holds **SOS** (1.5 s), or holds **Send SOS** on the missing-rider card;
- **crash detection**: 30-second countdown with "I'm OK" / "Need Help", then the SOS goes out by itself;
- **"Bike down?"**: the bike falls while stopped or at walking pace and does not move for a minute, which opens the same 30-second countdown.

When the SOS goes out:
- the group, the lead and the sweeper get the alert. The SOS alarm sound can break through Do Not Disturb where the phone allows it;
- the rider's emergency contact gets a text with a live link. The rider's phone sends it through the messages app if the internet is down. If CoRoute has server SMS turned on, the server can also send it;
- the operator console shows the red banner.

### 4.2 Who does what

| Step | Lead | Sweeper | Operator / base |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Stop the group safely off the road. Do not stop in a blind bend. | If behind the rider, stop and secure the scene first (hazard lights, warn traffic). | Read the banner: rider, ride, time. Open **Emergency contact**. |
| 2 | Send the nearest one or two riders back (or the sweeper). Not the whole group. | Check the rider: conscious? breathing? bleeding? | **Call 112** if the riders on scene have not, and give the last position from the dialog. |
| 3 | Open the incident card in the app: **nearest hospital** with Navigate, and the live emergency link for the lead. | **Call 112** (or 108 for an ambulance) from the incident screen. Give the location: the live link or coordinates. | Call the **emergency contact** (from the dialog) when appropriate, calm and factual. |
| 4 | Keep the rest of the group together and off the road. | Do not move the rider unless there is danger. Do not remove the helmet unless trained. Keep them warm. | Share the **live link** with family or responders if needed. Note the times. |
| 5 | When help arrives: decide whether the ride continues, and set new roles (**Roles** in the console, or in the app). | Stay with the rider, or hand over to the support vehicle. | Watch the other rides on the map. Keep a written incident log. |
| 6 | The rider, or someone helping, closes the SOS ("Help reached me" / "False alarm"). | | The emergency contact view closes automatically when the SOS is resolved. |

### 4.3 Notes
- **Call 112 first** in any life-threatening situation. CoRoute does not call emergency services for you. The SMS text itself tells the contact "Call 112".
- **Riders from other groups nearby** may be asked to help when the rider needs it. Accepted responders see the first name, bike, medical info and emergency contact while the alert is open.
- **No signal**: the SOS is kept on the phone and sent as soon as there is a connection. Nearby CoRoute phones can relay it over the phone-to-phone link.
- **False alarm**: the rider taps **I'm OK** during the countdown. If the SOS has already gone out, the rider closes it with **False alarm**, and the contact gets a "safe now" text (when server SMS is on).
- **Privacy**: open the emergency contact only for that SOS. Each view is recorded. Do not copy medical info anywhere else.

## 5. After the ride

- [ ] **Arrival roll call**: confirm "Everyone has arrived" in the app. Guardians get "Arrived safely".
- [ ] The lead ends the ride in the app, or base presses **End** in the console.
- [ ] Check **Attendance**: any rider marked **Left early**? Confirm they are safe.
- [ ] Review the **incidents** for the ride (type, time, outcome) and complete your own incident report. CoRoute reports contain no locations.
- [ ] Export the CSV if your records need it (if it is in your plan). Store it securely, and delete it when you no longer need it.
- [ ] Debrief the lead and sweeper: what worked, near misses, route changes for next time.
- [ ] Send a **broadcast** with thanks and the next ride (no prices, offers, phone numbers or e-mail).
