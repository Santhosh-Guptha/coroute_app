The load balancer sends everything to vm1 while vm1 is healthy; vm2 is the backup. Caddy on vm1 sends website, admin and payment routes to vm2. vm1 and vm2 keep identical in-memory rides over an encrypted mirror link, so a failover loses nothing. vm3 and vm4 reach the database over its public endpoint with an allowlist.
