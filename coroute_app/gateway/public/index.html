Why riders choose CoRoute

Six things CoRoute does on every ride.

Built for Indian highways, ghats and high passes, where a group spreads out, the signal comes and goes and help can be far away.

  • Crash detection with a 30-second check

    A hard fall starts a loud alarm and a 30-second countdown, even over the lock screen. Tap I'm OK and nobody is disturbed. If you do not answer, your group and emergency contacts are alerted with your location.

  • SOS that works without signal

    No mobile data? Your phone texts your location by SMS. No network at all? Group phones on a shared hotspot pass the SOS along, phone to phone, until one gets through. Once it reaches us, our server also texts your emergency contacts.

  • Hears sirens when your earbuds don't

    Noise-cancelling earbuds, a helmet set or music can hide an ambulance. During a ride CoRoute listens for ambulance, police and fire sirens on the phone and tells you, in your earbuds, to give way.

  • Live intercom, hands-free, clean voice

    Talk to the whole group or one rider, push-to-talk or hands-free, through your helmet headset or the phone. Voice cleanup takes out wind and breath. No extra hardware, and nothing is recorded.

  • Family follows live, no app needed

    Guardians open a private link in any browser. They see the level you choose, hear about an SOS or a long unexpected stop, and get a message when you arrive. The link ends with the ride.

  • Your data is encrypted, never sold or shared

    Everything travels encrypted, passwords are stored as one-way hashes, and your location goes only to your group during a ride. No ads, no trackers, no data sale. How we protect it

    Safety first

    Built for the moment nobody plans for.

    Most rides end with chai and a group photo. CoRoute is built for the one that does not, and stays quiet the rest of the time.

    • Crash detection

      Runs on your phone through the whole ride, even with the screen locked. A hard impact and a fall start a loud alarm and a 30-second check. If you do not answer, help is asked for. Milder alarms learn from your "I'm OK" taps on each bike; a severe impact always alarms.

    • SOS to your group

      Hold to send. Your riders hear an alarm and get a route back to you, with the nearest hospital in the alert.

    • Guardians at home

      Family follow on a private link in any browser, no app needed. They hear about an SOS or a long unexpected stop.

    • Medical ID

      Blood group, allergies and an emergency contact can show on your lock screen, only while your SOS is open. Off by default.

    • Offline maps

      Save the map along your route on Wi-Fi the night before. It still shows on the high passes, with no signal at all.

    • Fuel and essentials

      A reminder before the reserve, and fuel, puncture repair, food and medical help along the road ahead.

    • Re-routing

      Missed a turn? CoRoute notices you are off route and finds a new way back to the group's plan.

    Built for the group

    Day five, 400 km from home, no signal for an hour. Still together.

    Long rides break groups apart: a slow climb, a missed turn, a fuel stop nobody planned. These are the tools that keep a touring group together from the first morning to the last.

    Every feature, before, during and after the ride

    • Live group map

      Every rider on one map, ordered by road distance. The lead and the sweeper hear when someone drops behind, goes quiet or runs low on battery.

    • Live intercom

      Hold to talk, or go hands-free, to the whole group or one rider through your helmet headset or phone. Wind and breath are cleaned out, and voice is never recorded.

    • Regroup

      One tap asks the group for a short stop, and planned regroup points keep everyone on the same plan when the signal drops.

    • Routes and stops, planned once

      Set the destination and the fuel, food and rest stops before anyone rolls off. Break reminders suggest a stop after about two hours.

    • Public rides

      Turn on public ride and riders nearby can see a rough spot, heading and group size, never names or exact positions. Say hi, ask for help or warn about the road with short, filtered messages. Off unless the host turns it on.

    Safe

    If something goes wrong, your people know.

    Crash detection runs on your phone through the whole ride, even with the screen locked. If it notices a hard fall, the alarm starts. If you do not answer within 30 seconds, help is asked for.

    1. Crash detected

      The phone's motion sensors notice a hard impact and fall. Nothing leaves your phone yet.

    2. 30 seconds to answer

      A loud alarm and a full-screen countdown, even over the lock screen. Dropped the phone? One tap and nobody is disturbed.

    3. SOS, signal or not

      Your riders hear an alarm and get a route back to you. With no mobile data your phone texts your location by SMS, group phones on a shared hotspot pass the alert along, and our server texts your emergency contacts once it arrives.

    4. Guardians follow live

      Family you add as guardians are notified and can follow on a private link in any browser, no app needed. The link stops when the ride ends.

    Also: hold-to-send SOS at any time, the nearest hospital in the alert, and guardians told about a long unexpected stop.

    CoRoute complements emergency services. It does not replace them. In an emergency in India, call 112 first.

    Your data is protected

    Your location is for your group. Nobody else.

    A touring app knows where you are for days at a time. We treat that seriously, and we say plainly who sees what.

    • Only during a ride. Live location is collected while you are in an active ride and shared only with that group.
    • Links that expire. Guardian links can be paused or revoked, and emergency links stop after 30 minutes or when the alert closes.
    • Voice is never recorded. Intercom voice is relayed live to your group and goes out only while you talk. Siren listening happens on your phone and nothing from it is sent.
    • Encrypted, never sold. Everything travels over HTTPS, passwords are stored as one-way hashes, and there are no ads, no trackers and no sale of data.
    • Yours to delete. Recorded routes are deleted after 90 days, and you can delete your account at any time in the app or on the account deletion page.

    Questions riders ask

    Before you plan the next tour.

    More answers on how it works and the safety page.

    Does every rider in the group need the app?

    Yes. Each rider needs CoRoute on an Android phone and the ride code or invite link. Up to __MAX_RIDERS__ riders can join one group. Family at home do not need the app: they follow a guardian link in a browser.

    What happens where there is no mobile signal?

    The map keeps working if you saved it for your route, and crash detection keeps running because it works on the phone. Your route is kept and sent when the signal returns, and an SOS keeps retrying. Live positions and the intercom need data, so on long dead stretches agree on regroup points before you lose signal.

    Will a pothole or a dropped phone send an SOS?

    Crash detection looks for a hard impact followed by a fall, which ordinary bumps do not produce. If it ever fires by mistake, you have 30 seconds to tap I'm OK and nothing is sent. If an alert did go out, mark it resolved and everyone who was told sees that.

    Who can see where I am?

    Your ride group during the ride, and the guardians you add, at the level you choose. Nobody else. Emergency links show only your first name and last position and expire after 30 minutes. See the privacy policy for the details.

    How is my data protected?

    Everything travels encrypted over HTTPS, passwords are stored as one-way hashes, voice is relayed live and never recorded, and your medical info is shown only during an emergency. We never sell your data or share it with advertisers. You can delete your account at any time. The plain-language details are on your data is protected.

    How is this different from sharing live location in a chat?

    Chat live location shows each person in a separate bubble and expires after a set time. CoRoute puts the whole group on one map until the ride ends, warns the lead and sweeper when someone falls behind, adds a live intercom, and watches for crashes and sirens. If something goes wrong, the SOS still tries SMS and nearby group phones when there is no data.

    How are the reviews on this page chosen?

    After a trip, the app asks for a rating, a few words and, if you like, a photo of your group. Only riders who tick the box to allow publishing can appear here, and the CoRoute team reads every story and looks at every photo before it goes live. Photo location data is removed when you upload. We never add stories or ratings of our own.

    Is it free?

    Yes for solo riders, always. Solo riding and every safety feature are free: crash detection and SOS, siren alerts, guardians, Medical ID, offline maps, trip reports and your ride analytics. Joining a group ride you are invited to is free too. For hosting group rides, see the plans page. There are no ads.

    What do the numbers on this site say about me?

    Nothing. They are totals from completed rides, rounded down, and a number only appears once at least ten riders or rides are behind it. There is nothing per city, per route or per rider. The site sets no cookies and loads no trackers. Your own ride analytics live in the app, for you only.
