How it works

Four steps to the first ride.

  1. Install and sign up

    Download the Android app. Add your bike, an emergency contact and your medical ID.

  2. Create or join a ride

    One rider creates the ride and sets the stops. Others join with the code or link.

  3. Ride

    The map, the intercom and the alerts run with the phone in your pocket.

  4. Look back

    The ride lands in your timeline with the route, the stops and what happened.

If something goes wrong

What happens on a crash.

If the phone detects a hard fall, a countdown starts. If you do not cancel, your group gets an alarm with your position, your guardians get a notification, and, if you switched it on, an emergency text goes from your SIM. Riders of other Coroute groups on the same road may be asked to help. Call 112 first.

Your group

Alarm, position and a route to you.

Your guardians

A notification and a live link.

Nearby riders

Only if they are already on your road, and only if you allow it.

False alarm?

Cancel in the countdown, or mark it resolved afterwards.

How safety works, in detail

Questions

Short answers.

Is Coroute free?

The Community Rider plan is free and has no ads. Paid plans add more and are billed by UPI.

How many riders can join one ride?

Up to __MAX_RIDERS__ riders in one ride group.

Is there an iPhone version?

Coroute is Android only. No iOS version yet.

Does it work without mobile data?

Positions and the intercom need data. When the signal drops, the route is kept on the phone and sent later, an SOS waits and retries, and the SMS and mesh options cover the gaps.

Who can see my location?

Only the members of the ride you are in, while you are in it. Guardians and share links see it only when you turn them on. See the privacy policy.

Is the intercom recorded?

No. Voice is relayed live and discarded.
