How we count

  • Totals only. Counted from completed rides. Never per city, per route or per rider, and no times finer than the hour.

Safety first

Built for the moment nobody plans for.

Most rides end with chai and a group photo. CoRoute is built for the one that does not, and stays quiet the rest of the time.

  • Crash detection

    Runs on your phone through the whole ride, even with the screen locked. A hard impact and a fall start a loud alarm with 15 seconds to cancel. If you do not, help is asked for.

  • SOS to your group

    Hold to send. Your riders hear an alarm and get a route back to you, with the nearest hospital in the alert.

  • Guardians at home

    Family follow on a private link in any browser, no app needed. They hear about an SOS or a long unexpected stop.

  • Medical ID

    Blood group, allergies and an emergency contact can show on your lock screen, only while your SOS is open. Off by default.

  • Offline maps

    Save the map along your route on Wi-Fi the night before. It still shows on the high passes, with no signal at all.

  • Fuel and essentials

    A reminder before the reserve, and fuel, puncture repair, food and medical help along the road ahead.

  • Re-routing

    Missed a turn? CoRoute notices you are off route and finds a new way back to the group's plan.

Built for the group

Day five, 400 km from home, no signal for an hour. Still together.

Long rides break groups apart: a slow climb, a missed turn, a fuel stop nobody planned. These are the tools that keep a touring group together from the first morning to the last.

Every feature, before, during and after the ride

  • Live group map

    Every rider on one map, ordered by road distance. The lead and the sweeper hear when someone drops behind, goes quiet or runs low on battery.

  • Push-to-talk

    Hold to talk to the whole group through your helmet headset or phone. The microphone is off the rest of the time, and voice is never recorded.

  • Regroup

    One tap asks the group for a short stop, and planned regroup points keep everyone on the same plan when the signal drops.

  • Routes and stops, planned once

    Set the destination and the fuel, food and rest stops before anyone rolls off. Break reminders suggest a stop after about two hours.

  • Public rides

    Turn on public ride and riders nearby can see a rough spot, heading and group size, never names or exact positions. Say hi, ask for help or warn about the road with short, filtered messages. Off unless the host turns it on.

Safe

If something goes wrong, your people know.

Crash detection runs on your phone through the whole ride, even with the screen locked. If it notices a hard fall, the alarm starts. If you do not cancel it, help is asked for.

  1. Crash detected

    The phone's motion sensors notice a hard impact and fall. Nothing leaves your phone yet.

  2. 15 seconds to cancel

    A loud alarm and a full-screen countdown, even over the lock screen. Dropped the phone? One tap and nobody is disturbed.

  3. SOS to your group

    Your riders hear an alarm and get a route back to you. With no internet, CoRoute can open a ready-to-send text with your location.

  4. Guardians follow live

    Family you add as guardians are notified and can follow on a private link in any browser, no app needed. The link stops when the ride ends.

Also: hold-to-send SOS at any time, the nearest hospital in the alert, and guardians told about a long unexpected stop.

CoRoute complements emergency services. It does not replace them. In an emergency in India, call 112 first.

How safety works, step by step

Secure and private

Your location is for your group. Nobody else.

A touring app knows where you are for days at a time. We treat that seriously, and we say plainly who sees what.

  • Only during a ride. Live location is collected while you are in an active ride and shared only with that group.
  • Links that expire. Guardian links can be paused or revoked, and emergency links stop after 30 minutes or when the alert closes.
  • Microphone off by default. Voice goes out only while you hold Talk and is never recorded.
  • No ads, no trackers, no sale of data. Recorded routes are deleted after 90 days, and you can delete your account in the app at any time.

Your ride analytics

Know your riding, kilometre by kilometre.

CoRoute adds up your own rides into personal analytics: how far, how long, how fast and how safely you ride. They are built on your phone from your own rides, private to you, and free for every rider.

  • Distance and rides. Kilometres by week and month, rides completed and your longest day.
  • Hours in the saddle. Moving time, stops and how long your breaks really were.
  • Top speed and averages. Your fastest stretch and your average moving speed, ride by ride.
  • Safety stats. Hard braking counted by your phone, alerts and regroups, so you can see how you ride and improve.

Only you see them. They are never on this website, never used for ads and never sold.

    Questions riders ask

    Before you plan the next tour.

    More answers on how it works and the safety page.

    Does every rider in the group need the app?

    Yes. Each rider needs CoRoute on an Android phone and the ride code or invite link. Up to __MAX_RIDERS__ riders can join one group. Family at home do not need the app: they follow a guardian link in a browser.

    What happens where there is no mobile signal?

    The map keeps working if you saved it for your route, and crash detection keeps running because it works on the phone. Your route is kept and sent when the signal returns, and an SOS keeps retrying. Live positions and the intercom need data, so on long dead stretches agree on regroup points before you lose signal.

    Will a pothole or a dropped phone send an SOS?

    Crash detection looks for a hard impact followed by a fall, which ordinary bumps do not produce. If it ever fires by mistake, you have 15 seconds to cancel and nothing is sent. If an alert did go out, mark it resolved and everyone who was told sees that.

    Who can see where I am?

    Your ride group during the ride, and the guardians you add, at the level you choose. Nobody else. Emergency links show only your first name and last position and expire after 30 minutes. See the privacy policy for the details.

    How are the reviews on this page chosen?

    After a trip, the app asks for a rating, a few words and, if you like, a photo of your group. Only riders who tick the box to allow publishing can appear here, and the CoRoute team reads every story and looks at every photo before it goes live. Photo location data is removed when you upload. We never add stories or ratings of our own.

    Is it free?

    Yes for solo riders, always. The Community plan gives solo riders every safety feature free, forever: crash detection and SOS, guardians, Medical ID, offline maps, trip reports and your ride analytics. Joining a group ride you are invited to is free too. Only the host of a group ride needs a group plan, shown in the pricing section and read live from the app. There are no ads.

    What do the numbers on this site say about me?

    Nothing. They are totals from completed rides, rounded down, and a number only appears once at least ten riders or rides are behind it. There is nothing per city, per route or per rider. The site sets no cookies and loads no trackers. Your own ride analytics live in the app, for you only.
