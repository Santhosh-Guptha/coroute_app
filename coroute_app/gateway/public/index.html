Features

What CoRoute does

Made for a phone mounted on a motorcycle: large touch targets, high contrast, and voice for anything that cannot wait for a stop.

Live convoy map

Every rider's position, speed and heading on an OpenStreetMap view, together with the planned route and the distance between first and last rider.

Position updates while moving; heading from GPS and the phone compass.

Voice intercom

Hold to talk to everyone, or pick one rider for a private channel. The hands-free mode transmits only while you speak.

Live stream, no recordings. One speaker at a time on the group channel.

SOS that reaches everyone

One tap sends an alert with your position to the whole convoy and keeps it on every screen until someone marks it resolved.

Medical, breakdown, crash or a custom reason.

Stops and status cards

Quick cards for fuel, rest, photo stop or a two-minute regroup. Planned stops appear on the route and can be ticked off as you pass them.

Status cards, wait timer, planned waypoints.

Battery use

GPS updates slow down when you stop, audio is only sent while someone is talking, and the app keeps one connection open instead of polling.

Adaptive GPS, one connection, tracking continues with the screen off.

Ride history

Every convoy becomes a trip record: who rode, how far, how fast, and where you stopped. The route trace is kept for 90 days.

Summaries are kept; GPS trails are deleted after 90 days.

How it works

How a ride works

Create a convoy

The lead rider names the ride, sets the destination and receives a six-digit code such as 483 921 to share with the group.

Everyone joins with the code

Riders enter the code and appear on the map. Each rider sees the others, the route and the planned stops.

Ride, talk, regroup

Hold to talk or use the hands-free mode, send a fuel card when you stop, press SOS if something goes wrong. Ending the ride saves it for everyone.

Free and non-profit

Run as a public service for riders

CoRoute is made by riders at devmonks.space and runs on free-tier cloud infrastructure, so it costs nothing to use. There is no premium tier and no advertising.

  • Free forever for every rider and every group size.
  • No account selling, no analytics SDKs, no third-party trackers.
  • Feedback is welcome by e-mail; the address is in the footer.

Privacy, in plain words

Only your convoy sees you

  • Location is shared only with the convoy you are in, and only while you are in it. It stops when you leave.
  • Voice is not recorded. It is relayed live and discarded.
  • GPS traces are deleted after 90 days. Your account and ride summaries stay until you delete them.
  • Passwords are hashed; connections are encrypted; the app holds no database credentials.
