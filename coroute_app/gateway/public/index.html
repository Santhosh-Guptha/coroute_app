Live group awareness

Know where the group is without searching for it.

One map for the whole ride. Glance at it at a stop, or let the alerts speak while you ride.

  • Every rider live, with speed and heading
  • Distance ahead and behind you, and the group spread
  • Planned route, stops and arrival ticks
  • Off the route? It reroutes, and the road behind you clears
  • A large ride notification on the lock screen

Also in the safety tools

  • Hold to send SOS

    No pocket taps. Medical, breakdown, crash or your own reason.

  • Crash detection New in 3.15

    Asks "I'm OK" or "Need help" for 15 seconds. No answer sends the SOS.

  • No signal New in 3.15

    SOS waits and sends when you reconnect. If you switch it on, your phone texts your group and emergency contact.

  • "I'm going" New in 3.15

    Riders in your group can say they are on the way, so everyone knows who is coming.

Two separate switches

Who can help you is not who can see you.

Safety

Who can help you.

  • Your group is always alerted first
  • Up to 3 nearby riders may be asked, if you allow it
  • Only the spot and the distance until they say yes
  • Works in Public and Private

Social

Who can see your group.

  • Off until the lead sets the group to Public
  • Both groups have to be Public
  • Group name, rider count and a rough distance
  • No names, positions or phone numbers

How it works

Three steps to the same road.

  1. 01

    Create

    Name the ride, set the destination and add stops. You get a six-digit code.

  2. 02

    Join

    Riders enter the code or open the shared link. Up to __MAX_RIDERS__ riders in one ride.

  3. 03

    Ride

    Map, voice and safety run together until you end the ride. Then it is saved for everyone.
