Permissions

What CoRoute asks for, and why.

In plain words. Location and microphone are used only while you are in an active ride. Audio privacy is built in: microphone is OFF by default.

If Android switches a permission off during a ride, a clear banner tells you and opens the right settings screen in one tap.

Location Needed So your group can see where you are during a ride, and you can see them.
Location in the background Needed So your group still sees you with the phone in your pocket or mounted to the handlebar. Only during an active ride.
Microphone Off by default. Used only while you hold Talk, or after you switch on hands-free mode yourself. Voice is never recorded or stored.
Notifications For ride alerts, emergencies and the ride notification with the riders ahead and behind.
Run in the background Keeps GPS, the intercom and the connection to your group going with the screen locked. Android shows a notification while it runs.
Unrestricted battery use Some phones close apps in the background after a few minutes. This stops that during a long ride.
Full-screen alarm So the crash alarm and the hold-to-send SOS screen can open over the lock screen.
Emergency text No permission needed If your SOS cannot go over the internet, CoRoute opens your messages app with a ready-to-send text and your location. You send it from your own SIM.
Nearby devices (Bluetooth) Recommended Asked in the pre-ride check and by the rescue finder. It lets the group's phones pass messages along when there is no signal (the offline link), lets your phone send its rescue beacon after an unanswered crash, and routes voice to your helmet headset. It is not used to find your location. Without it, the siren and SOS by text still work.
Internet, network state, keep awake, vibrate The basics: talk to the server, notice when the signal drops, keep the ride running, and buzz for alerts.

The motion sensor for crash detection needs no permission. It is read on your phone only, during a ride. Spoken alerts use your phone's own text-to-speech.

Installing

Install from the download

The download is an APK file from this site. Android asks once before it installs apps from outside an app store.

  1. Tap Download for Android and wait for the file to finish.
  2. Open the downloaded file from the notification or your Downloads folder.
  3. If Android asks, allow your browser to install apps. You can switch this off again afterwards.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.
  5. Sign up, add your emergency contact and guardians, and run the pre-ride checklist.

iPhone and iPad

Installing on iOS

iOS devices install apps through Apple TestFlight, the App Store, or developer sideloading.

  1. Tap Download for iOS above to open the TestFlight or App Store installation page.
  2. If using TestFlight, install Apple TestFlight from the App Store and tap Start Testing.
  3. For offline IPA installation, install the package using Xcode or AltStore with your Apple ID.
  4. Open CoRoute, allow Location (select Always Allow for safety alarms while the phone is locked), Motion and Notifications.

Updates

When you need a new version, the app tells you and links to the download. Install it over the current app. Your account and your rides stay.

The newest version is always on this page.

Privacy

Your location is shared only with your ride group and the guardians you choose, only during the ride. Voice is never recorded. No ads, no trackers, no sale of data. Group plans are chosen and paid for on the plans page.
