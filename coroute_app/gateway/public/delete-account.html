Overview

You have full control over your personal data. You may delete your CoRoute account and all associated data at any time, either directly from within the mobile app or by submitting a request through this web portal. Deletion is irrevocable and permanently purges your personal records from our active databases.

Option 1: Delete Directly in the CoRoute App (Fastest)

You can instantly delete your account inside the mobile application:

  1. Open the CoRoute app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Profile tab on the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap Account & Security (or Settings).
  4. Scroll down and tap Delete My Account.
  5. Confirm the prompt. Your active session will terminate immediately and your data will be permanently wiped.

Option 2: Web Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you can submit an account deletion request via email or through the form below. We process web deletion requests within 24 to 48 hours.

What Data is Deleted?

When your account is deleted, the following records are permanently wiped from our systems:

  • Account Profile: User ID, name, email address, phone number, vehicle details, and avatar image.
  • Trip History & GPS Trails: All recorded routes, tracks, waypoints, convoy logs, and past telemetry.
  • Emergency Contacts & Medical ID: Guardian contact numbers, names, and user-provided emergency medical notes.
  • Social Connections: Friend relationships, crew memberships, and pending requests.
  • Reviews & Photos: Post-trip feedback, submitted road photos, and place ratings.
  • Device Identifiers: Firebase push tokens and temporary device session credentials.

Retention & Backups

Data deletion from active production databases occurs immediately upon action. Encrypted automated disaster-recovery snapshots are rotated out and completely expire within 30 days. No anonymized personal data or advertising identifiers are retained because CoRoute does not serve ads or sell user information.
