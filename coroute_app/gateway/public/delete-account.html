Overview

You have full control over your personal data. You may delete your CoRoute account and all associated data at any time, either directly from within the mobile app or by submitting a request through this web portal. Deletion is irrevocable and permanently purges your personal records from our active databases.

Option 1: Delete Directly in the CoRoute App (Fastest)

You can instantly delete your account inside the mobile application:

  1. Open the CoRoute app on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Profile tab on the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll down to the Account section.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm the prompt. Your account is deleted at once and you are signed out on this phone.

Option 2: Web Deletion Request

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, send us a deletion request. We complete deletion requests within 48 hours after checking that the request comes from the e-mail address registered with the account.

Submit Request to Support

Send an email to our support address with the subject line Delete My CoRoute Account:

Support Email: __SUPPORT_EMAIL__

Use the contact form on our home page: choose "Something else" and start your message with Delete My CoRoute Account.

Please include:

  • Your registered email address or phone number used in CoRoute (write from that e-mail address if you can).
  • Your username (if remembered).

Once verified, our team will purge your account and send a confirmation reply to your email address.

What Data is Deleted?

When your account is deleted, the following records are permanently wiped from our systems:

  • Account Profile: User ID, name, email address, phone number, vehicle details, and avatar image.
  • Trip History & GPS Trails: All recorded routes, tracks, waypoints, convoy logs, and past telemetry.
  • Emergency Contacts & Medical ID: Guardian contact numbers, names, and user-provided emergency medical notes.
  • Social Connections: Friend relationships, crew memberships, and pending requests.
  • Reviews & Photos: Post-trip feedback, submitted road photos, and place ratings.
  • Device Identifiers: Firebase push tokens and temporary device session credentials.
  • Diagnostics: app logs you sent to our support team.

Retention & Backups

Deleting in the app removes your data from our live database at once; a request sent to us is completed within 48 hours. Encrypted backup copies are rotated out and expire within 30 days. The rides you shared stay in the other riders' history with your name replaced by "Former rider". Payment records for website plan purchases (order, amount, payment id) are kept for accounting. CoRoute does not serve ads or sell user information, so no advertising identifiers are kept. More detail is in our Privacy Policy.
