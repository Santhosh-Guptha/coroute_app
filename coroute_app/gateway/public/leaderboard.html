    Your privacy

    Your name, your choice.

    • Username only

      Boards show the unique username you picked in the app. Never your real name, e-mail, phone, bike or photo.

    • Rounded totals

      Only rounded totals are shown. No routes, places, dates or times of any ride.

    • Hide your name

      Prefer to ride unlisted? Hide your name from the leaderboards in the app, and your username is left off every board.
