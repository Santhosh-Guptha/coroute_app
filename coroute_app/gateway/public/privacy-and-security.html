Our promises

Six things we never compromise on.

  • Encrypted on the way

    Every connection between the app, this website and our servers uses HTTPS (TLS), so nobody on the network can read it.

  • Passwords stored as hashes

    We never keep your password. We keep a one-way scrypt hash of it, which cannot be turned back into the password.

  • Location only during rides

    Your live position is collected during an active ride and shared only with that ride's group, and with guardians at the level you choose.

  • Voice relayed, never stored

    Intercom voice goes live to your group and is gone. Siren listening runs on your phone and sends nothing.

  • Medical info only in emergencies

    Your Medical ID is shown to your group and guardians only while your SOS or crash alert is open. Our staff cannot see it.

  • No ads, no trackers, no sale

    No advertising, no third-party trackers or analytics in the app or on this site, and we never sell or rent your data.

How we keep it safe

Security practices, as built.

These are the protections in CoRoute's servers and apps today. No system is perfect; if you find a problem, tell us at the address on the privacy policy.

  • HTTPS everywhere

    Every page and connection is served over TLS, and browsers are told to always use HTTPS (HSTS).

  • Strong password hashing

    Passwords are hashed with scrypt; older hashes are upgraded the next time you sign in.

  • Links that cannot be guessed

    Guardian and emergency links are long random codes that we store only as hashes. Emergency links stop after 30 minutes.

  • Rate limits

    Sign-in, ride codes, links and public pages are rate limited, which slows down anyone trying to guess or flood them.

  • Signed payment messages

    Payment confirmations from our payment provider are checked against a secret signature before anything changes.

  • Admin audit log

    Every change our staff make to a rider's account, and every view of a diagnostics log, is recorded. Staff cannot see medical info or listen to voice.

  • Encrypted backups, stored in India

    Our database runs in a cloud data centre in Hyderabad, India. Backups are encrypted with AES-256 before they are stored.

  • No trackers on this site

    This website loads no outside fonts, trackers or analytics, and uses a strict content security policy. The only outside script is the payment provider's checkout, and only when you pay on the plans page.

Your control

Your data, your call.

  • Delete at any time. Delete your account in the app (Profile, Account, Delete account) and it is removed at once, or ask on the account deletion page and we complete it within 48 hours. Your profile, trips, routes and contacts are removed; backup copies expire within 30 days.
  • Choose who follows. Pause or revoke a guardian link whenever you like. Emergency links end after 30 minutes or when the alert closes.
  • Choose the extras. Siren alerts, voice alerts, notifications and leaderboards each have a switch. Crash detection, SOS, emergency texts and help from nearby riders are always on, for your safety; nearby riders see only the emergency point until one accepts.
  • Hide your name. Keep your username off the public leaderboards with one switch.
  • Ask for a copy. You can ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it.

Designed with India's DPDP Act in mind

CoRoute is designed with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 in mind: we collect data for a stated purpose, ask before we use the sensitive parts, keep it only as long as needed, and let you see, correct and erase it. This is our own description of how we work, not a certification.

Questions, corrections or complaints about your data: contact us through the privacy policy.
