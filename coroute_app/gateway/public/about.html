Principles

Clear and open by design.

  • Free to join a ride

    Hosts pay, riders do not

  • No ads, no trackers

    In the app and on this site

  • No sale of data

    Never, to anyone

  • Mic off by default

    Voice is never stored

  • Honest about limits

    In India, call 112 first

  • Real reviews only

    Checked, never written by us
