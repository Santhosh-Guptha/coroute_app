About

Built because riders needed it.

Coroute is a free app for group motorcycle rides, made by riders. It keeps a group connected on the road and helps it look out for each other.

Source on GitHub Send feedback

The idea

The idea

Ride together. Stay connected. Look out for each other.

Ride together

A group ride is one ride, not a set of separate trips. Coroute puts everyone on one map, on one route, with one plan for the stops.

Stay connected

Talk without stopping and without a phone call. Know who is ahead, who is behind, and who has lost signal.

Look out for each other

When something goes wrong, the people closest to you should know first. How safety works.

Who makes it

A public service, not a business.

Non-profit

Coroute is run as a public service for riders. There are no investors, no premium tier and no advertising.

Made by riders

It is built and maintained by riders at devmonks.space, as volunteers.

Free to use

It runs on free-tier cloud infrastructure, so it costs nothing to use. Every feature is free for every rider.

Open source

Read the code.

The app and the server are on GitHub. You can see how Coroute works, what it sends and what it keeps.

Promises

What Coroute will never do.

Built for riders. Not advertisers.

Show ads

Not in the app, not on this site. Not now, not later.

Sell your data

Your rides, your location and your contacts are not for sale. To anyone.

Add tracking SDKs

No third-party analytics or tracking code in the app. This site sets no cookies and loads nothing from other servers.

Record your voice

The intercom is relayed live and discarded. It is never stored.

Share your location outside your group

Only your ride group sees you, only during the ride. In an emergency you raise, helpers from other groups see the minimum. Privacy policy.

Charge riders

No subscription and no paid tier.

Where it stands

An early project. Honestly.

Coroute is young and made by volunteers. Some things are missing, and some will change.
