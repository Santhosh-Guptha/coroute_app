About

Built because riders needed it.

Coroute is made by riders at DevMonks. It keeps a group connected on the road and helps it look out for each other.

Get the app Send feedback

Principles

Clear and open by design.

  • Free to start

    Community Rider costs nothing. Paid plans fund the servers.

  • No ads, no trackers

    No advertising and no tracking SDKs in the app or this site.

  • No sale of data

    Your location goes to your group only, while you ride.

  • No voice recording

    The intercom is relayed live and discarded.

  • Honest about limits

    Coroute helps people know. It cannot promise that anyone comes.

  • Made by riders

    Built and tested on real group rides by DevMonks.
