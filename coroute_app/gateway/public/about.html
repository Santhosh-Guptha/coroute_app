About

Built because riders needed it.

CoRoute is made by riders at DevMonks. It started with a simple problem every touring group knows: on a long ride, somebody always falls behind, the signal goes, and nobody is sure where they are.

Get the app Send feedback

Principles

Clear and open by design.

  • Free to join a ride

    Invited riders join free of charge. Memberships for the rider who hosts a ride pay for running the service and for safety work.

  • No ads, no trackers

    No advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, in the app or on this website.

  • No sale of data

    Your location goes to your ride group during the ride, and to the guardians you choose. It is never sold.

  • Microphone off by default

    The intercom transmits only while push-to-talk is held. Voice is never stored.

  • Honest about limits

    CoRoute complements emergency services; it does not replace them. In an emergency in India, call 112 first.

  • Real reviews only

    Rider stories on this site come from completed trips and are checked by the CoRoute team before they appear. We never write our own.
