1. What CoRoute is

CoRoute is a mobile application that helps a group of riders stay together: it shares each member's position with the other members of the same convoy, provides a live voice intercom, quick status messages and SOS alerts, and keeps a history of rides. Invited riders join convoys free of charge, supported by transparent Trip Owner memberships with zero advertising.

2. Accounts and Convoy Roles

You need an account (e-mail and password, or Google Sign-In) to use CoRoute. You must be at least 18 years old. Keep your password confidential and tell us if you believe your account has been used by someone else. You are responsible for activity on your account. We may suspend accounts that abuse the service or other riders.

A convoy operates with two designated coordination roles: the Trip Owner (the rider who creates the convoy, holds the membership plan that sets how many riders can join, manages invites, and handles membership billing) and the Trip Head (the lead rider responsible for route navigation, pace, waypoints, and on-road group decisions). Invited riders participate free of charge.

3. Memberships and Billing

CoRoute offers transparent, capacity-based membership plans managed directly inside the mobile app. Memberships cover operational server infrastructure, safety relay systems, and map services. The plans are Individual (solo trips), Small Group (up to 6 riders) and Large Group (up to 12 riders). Joining a ride you are invited to is free, and safety features (including offline crash detection and emergency alerts) are never behind a plan. If a Trip Owner's membership expires during an active ride, ongoing trip tracking and emergency safety services are never interrupted until the ride concludes.

4. Riding safety

CoRoute is an aid to group coordination, not a safety device. It depends on your phone, its GPS and its mobile network connection, any of which can fail or be delayed. Always ride within the law and your abilities, keep your eyes on the road, and interact with the app only when it is safe to do so. In an emergency contact the local emergency services directly; an SOS alert in CoRoute reaches the members of your convoy first, and may also be sent to a few riders of other groups who are already riding toward you on the same road, with only the information described in the Privacy Policy, unless you turn off "Ask nearby riders to help me".

5. Acceptable use

Do not use CoRoute to harass, threaten or stalk anyone, to share unlawful content over the intercom or chat, to track a person without their knowledge and consent, or to interfere with the service or other users. Do not attempt to access other convoys or other riders' data. We may remove content, dissolve convoys and suspend accounts that break these rules.
