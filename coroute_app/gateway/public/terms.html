CoRoute Terms of Use

Effective 2 October 2026. CoRoute is a free, non-profit service operated by devmonks.space.

1. What CoRoute is

CoRoute is a mobile application that helps a group of riders stay together: it shares each member's position with the other members of the same convoy, provides a live voice intercom, quick status messages and SOS alerts, and keeps a history of rides. It is provided free of charge and without advertising.

2. Accounts

You need an account (e-mail and password, or Google Sign-In) to use CoRoute. You must be at least 18 years old. Keep your password confidential and tell us if you believe your account has been used by someone else. You are responsible for activity on your account. We may suspend accounts that abuse the service or other riders.

3. Riding safety

CoRoute is an aid to group coordination, not a safety device. It depends on your phone, its GPS and its mobile network connection, any of which can fail or be delayed. Always ride within the law and your abilities, keep your eyes on the road, and interact with the app only when it is safe to do so. In an emergency contact the local emergency services directly; an SOS alert in CoRoute reaches only the members of your convoy.

4. Acceptable use

Do not use CoRoute to harass, threaten or stalk anyone, to share unlawful content over the intercom or chat, to track a person without their knowledge and consent, or to interfere with the service or other users. Do not attempt to access other convoys or other riders' data. We may remove content, dissolve convoys and suspend accounts that break these rules.

5. Your content

Messages, status cards and ride names you create remain yours. You grant us the limited right to transmit and store them as needed to run the service for you and your convoy. Voice is relayed live and is not stored.
