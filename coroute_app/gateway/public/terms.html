1. What CoRoute is

CoRoute is a mobile application that helps a group of riders stay together: it shares each member's position with the other members of the same convoy, provides a live voice intercom, quick status messages and SOS alerts, and keeps a history of rides. Joining a ride you are invited to is free, solo riding is free, and safety features are never part of a plan. CoRoute shows no ads.

2. Accounts and Convoy Roles

You need an account (e-mail and password, or Google Sign-In) to use CoRoute. You must be at least 18 years old. Keep your password confidential and tell us if you believe your account has been used by someone else. You are responsible for activity on your account. We may suspend accounts that abuse the service or other riders.

Each ride has these roles:

  • Trip owner: the rider who created the ride. The trip owner invites riders, removes riders and ends the trip, and their plan (if any) sets how many riders can join.
  • Trip head (optional): a second owner chosen by the trip owner, who can change the trip's settings only. A trip head cannot remove riders or end the trip, and is not the Lead unless also given that role.
  • Lead: a riding role, the rider at the front who guides the group on the road. The trip owner or the Lead runs the ride on the road (stops, route, regrouping). The Sweeper rides at the back.

Riders you invite join free of charge.

3. Plans, payments and refunds

Group plans (for example Small Group and Large Group) set how many riders a trip owner can host. Plans are shown and bought only on our website at /plans, not in the app. Prices are the ones shown on that page when you pay, and payments are handled by Razorpay. A plan runs for the period shown at purchase and does not renew on its own, so there is nothing to cancel. While a plan is active you can only move to a bigger plan; the unused days of your current plan are credited against the new price.

CoRoute may make hosting group rides free for everyone, or free up to a limit, for a time. A plan you already paid for keeps running unchanged when that happens. Joining a ride you are invited to is always free, and safety features (including crash detection, SOS and emergency texts) are never part of a plan. If a plan ends during a ride, the ride and its safety features continue until the ride ends.

Refunds and cancellations follow our Refund and Cancellation Policy.

4. Riding safety

CoRoute assists you and your group: it shares positions, raises alerts and helps others reach you. It is not a certified safety device and it does not replace emergency services. It depends on your phone, its GPS, its sensors and its mobile network, any of which can fail or be delayed, and crash detection can miss a crash or raise a false alarm. Always ride within the law and your abilities, keep your eyes on the road, and interact with the app only when it is safe to do so. In an emergency in India, call 112 (108 for an ambulance) first. An SOS alert in CoRoute reaches the members of your convoy first, and may also be sent to a few riders of other groups who are already riding toward you on the same road, with only the information described in the Privacy Policy. Riders who answer an alert are volunteers, not professional responders.

5. Acceptable use

Do not attempt to access other convoys or other riders' data. We may remove content, dissolve convoys and suspend accounts that break these rules.

An operator, not CoRoute, organises its rides and is responsible for them, including its briefings, routes and any fees it charges. Operators see only what the Privacy Policy describes; a member's emergency contact is shown to them only during that member's open SOS, and they may use it only to help in that emergency. We may suspend an operator that misuses the service or riders' data.
