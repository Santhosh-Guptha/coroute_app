Quick start

Set up in ten minutes. Ride safer every time.

Before your first ride: install the app, give it the permissions it needs, add the people to call in an emergency, add your bike, then ride.

Important

  • In an emergency, call 112 first. CoRoute alerts your group and your emergency contact, but it does not replace emergency services.
  • Every safety feature is free for every rider: crash detection, SOS alerts to your group and your emergency contact, and a check-in when you have not arrived on time.
  • Joining a group ride is always free.

1. Install and sign in

  1. Install CoRoute on your Android phone (Get the app).
  2. Open it and sign in with your Google account, or with an e-mail and password.
  3. CoRoute gives you a username (for example SwiftFalcon482). Other riders see you by this name. You can change it later in Profile > My profile.

You must be 18 or older to use CoRoute.

2. Allow the permissions (and why)

CoRoute asks for each permission when it needs it. Each one has a safety reason:

PermissionWhy CoRoute needs it
Location: Allow all the timeYour group sees you and crash detection keeps working with the screen off or the phone in your pocket. Used only during a ride.
NotificationsThe ride notification, the crash countdown, SOS alerts from your group and friend or crew invites.
MicrophoneGroup talk (intercom) and Siren alerts. Only during a ride. Nothing is recorded.
Nearby devices / BluetoothVoice alerts and group talk through your helmet headset or earbuds.
Full-screen alerts (Android 14+)Shows the crash countdown over the lock screen so you can answer without unlocking.
Battery: UnrestrictedStops the phone from closing CoRoute in the middle of a long ride.

Then open Profile > Settings > Safety health. It checks your phone and shows a fix button for anything that is missing.

3. Add your emergency contact and Medical ID

  1. Go to Profile > Emergency contact & Medical ID. The row is amber until a contact is saved.
  2. Enter your emergency contact: name and phone number. CoRoute alerts this person if you send an SOS or crash.
  3. Optional but useful: blood group, allergies and notes for a doctor. These are shown on your lock screen and to the people helping you, only while your SOS or crash alert is open.
  4. Tap Save.

4. Add your bike

  1. Go to Profile > My bikes and tap Add bike.
  2. Enter the make and model, pick the bike type and colour, and fill in engine size, tank and mileage (for an electric bike: mileage only).
  3. Tap Save to garage.

Your group sees your bike on the map in its type and colour. Riding on someone else's bike? Choose I'm riding pillion (no bike) when you join.

5. Start a solo ride

  1. On Home, tap Start Ride. Pick a Destination (and stops if you like), name the trip and tap Confirm and Create Trip.
  2. Tap Start the ride and go. Crash detection starts once you are moving, and the screen stays on.
  3. No signal where you are going? Use Ride solo without internet: crash detection and SOS by text keep working on your phone.

With a destination and an emergency contact set, CoRoute also checks on you if you have not arrived by your expected time.

6. Create or join a group ride

Create a ride

  1. On Home, tap Plan a route. Set the Destination, add stops if you like, give the trip a name, and tap Confirm and Create Trip.
  2. Share the ride code with your riders (or invite your crew from People).
  3. When everyone is in, tap Start the ride.

Join a ride

  1. On Home, tap Join Ride and enter the code the trip owner shared. Or open a Ride invitation and tap Accept.
  2. Joining a group ride is always free.

If you cannot host a group ride, the app says so plainly. Group hosting is managed in your CoRoute account on this website. Solo riding and every safety feature are free.

7. Remember on every ride

  • SOS: press and hold the red SOS button for 3 seconds until the ring is full.
  • Crash countdown: you have 30 seconds to tap I'm OK. If you do not answer, your group and emergency contact are alerted.
  • Phone in a handlebar mount gives the best crash and siren detection. Keep it charged.
