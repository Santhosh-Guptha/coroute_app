Reporting a security issue

We welcome reports from security researchers and riders. If you think you found a vulnerability in the CoRoute app, this website or our API, please tell us at __SECURITY_EMAIL__through the contact form on our home page (choose "Something went wrong" and start with "Security"). Include the steps to reproduce, the impact you expect and how we can reach you. Please do not include other people's personal data.

We will acknowledge your report and keep you informed while we fix it. Issues that could affect SOS, crash alerts, live locations or medical info come first. Please give us reasonable time to fix an issue before you tell anyone else about it.

In scope

  • The CoRoute Android app
  • The CoRoute API and real-time connection
  • This website, including the account, plans and live emergency link pages
  • The admin and operator sign-in pages

Out of scope

  • Denial-of-service or load testing
  • Social engineering of our team or riders, and physical attacks
  • Third-party services (Razorpay, Google, Oracle Cloud, SMS providers): please report those to them
  • Automated scanner reports without a demonstrated impact, and missing best-practice headers without an exploit

Rules for testing

  • Test only with your own accounts.
  • Never raise a real SOS that reaches people who are not part of your test.
  • Never access, change or keep other riders' data. Stop and tell us as soon as you reach any personal data.

We do not run a paid bounty at this time. We are happy to thank you by name if you would like.
