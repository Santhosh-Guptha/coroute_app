Overview

This policy covers CoRoute group plans bought on our website at /plans. Plans are not sold in the CoRoute app, so there are no in-app purchases to refund. Solo riding, joining a ride you are invited to and every safety feature are free and need no plan.

No automatic renewal

A plan runs for the period shown when you buy it and then ends. It does not renew on its own and we never charge you again without you buying again, so there is nothing to cancel. Your account page shows your plan, its end date and your payments.

Changing plans

While a plan is active you can move only to a bigger plan. The unused days of your current plan are taken off the price of the new one, and the new plan starts at once. You cannot move to a smaller plan while your current plan is active.

At times CoRoute may make hosting group rides free for everyone, or free up to a limit. A plan you already bought keeps running unchanged when that happens; it is not shortened, cancelled or refunded because of it.

How to ask for a refund

Contact us at __SUPPORT_EMAIL__through the contact form on our home page with:

  • the order number (it is on your receipt and on your account page),
  • the e-mail address of your CoRoute account (write from it if you can), and
  • why you are asking for a refund.

If you were charged but your plan did not start, tell us as well: we check the payment with Razorpay and either start your plan or refund it. We reply within a few working days.

How refunds work

  • Refunds are made through Razorpay, back to the card, UPI account or bank account you paid with. How long the money takes to reach you depends on your bank or payment provider.
  • A full refund ends the plan it paid for at once.
  • A partial refund keeps the plan running until its end date.
  • Your receipt and account page show the refund, and we send you a refund e-mail when e-mail is set up.
