How it works

Install. Join. Ride. Look back.

Four steps from download to trip report. Set it up once at home, and the whole group rides with it, from the first morning to the last stop.

Get the app Short answers

The four steps

  1. 1. Install and sign up

    Download the app, sign up with your phone number or Google, and add your bike, an emergency contact, your medical ID and the guardians who should follow your rides.

  2. 2. Create or join

    One rider creates the ride, sets the destination and stops, and shares the code. Others join with the code or the link. Save the map for the route while you are on Wi-Fi.

  3. 3. Ride

    Start the ride. The live map, the intercom, crash detection, curve warnings, fuel and break reminders run while the phone is mounted or in your pocket. If the signal drops, the group's phones pass messages along.

  4. 4. Look back

    End the ride. The trip report and replay are ready, guardian links stop working, and you can rate the trip and share your story.

Questions

Short answers.

Does every rider in the group need the app?

Yes. Each rider needs CoRoute on an Android phone and the ride code or invite link. Up to __MAX_RIDERS__ riders can join one group. Family at home do not need the app: they follow a guardian link in a browser.

How many riders can join one ride?

Up to __MAX_RIDERS__ riders in one ride group.

What happens where there is no mobile signal?

The map keeps working if you saved it for your route, and crash detection keeps running because it works on the phone. Your route is kept and sent when the signal returns, and an SOS keeps retrying. Live positions and the intercom need data, so on long dead stretches agree on regroup points before you lose signal.

Will a pothole or a dropped phone send an SOS?

Crash detection looks for a hard impact followed by a fall, which ordinary bumps do not produce. If it ever fires by mistake, you have 30 seconds to tap I'm OK and nothing is sent. If an alert did go out, mark it resolved and everyone who was told sees that.

Who can see where I am?

Your ride group during the ride, and the guardians you add, at the level you choose. Nobody else. Emergency links show only your first name and last position and expire after 30 minutes. See the privacy policy for the details.

How is my data protected?

Everything travels encrypted over HTTPS, passwords are stored as one-way hashes, voice is relayed live and never recorded, and your medical info is shown only during an emergency. We never sell your data or share it with advertisers. You can delete your account at any time. The plain-language details are on your data is protected.

How is this different from sharing live location in a chat?

Chat live location shows each person in a separate bubble and expires after a set time. CoRoute puts the whole group on one map until the ride ends, warns the lead and sweeper when someone falls behind, adds a live intercom, and watches for crashes and sirens. If something goes wrong, the SOS still tries SMS and nearby group phones when there is no data.

How are the rider reviews on the home page chosen?

After a trip, the app asks for a rating, a few words and, if you like, a photo of your group. Only riders who tick the box to allow publishing can appear here, and the CoRoute team reads every story and looks at every photo before it goes live. Photo location data is removed when you upload. We never add stories or ratings of our own.

Is it free?

Yes for solo riders, always. Solo riding and every safety feature are free: crash detection and SOS, siren alerts, guardians, Medical ID, offline maps, trip reports and your ride analytics. Joining a group ride you are invited to is free too. For hosting group rides, see the plans page. There are no ads.

What do the numbers on this site say about me?

Nothing. They are totals from completed rides, rounded down, and a number only appears once at least ten riders or rides are behind it. There is nothing per city, per route or per rider. The site sets no cookies and loads no trackers. Your own ride analytics live in the app, for you only.

Does CoRoute replace calling for help?

No. In an emergency in India, call 112 first. CoRoute tells your group, your guardians and nearby riders of other groups. It cannot promise that anyone will come. See how safety works.
