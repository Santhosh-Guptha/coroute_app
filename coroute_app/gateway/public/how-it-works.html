Questions

Short answers.

Is it free?

Yes for solo riders, always: every safety feature, free forever. Joining a ride you are invited to is free too. Only the host of a group ride needs a plan, bought in the app. No ads.

How many riders can join one ride?

Up to __MAX_RIDERS__ riders in one ride group.

Does every rider need the app?

Yes, each rider needs it. Family follow a guardian link in a browser, no app needed.

Is there an iPhone version?

Yes. Get it from the App Store or TestFlight via the Get the app page. It needs iOS 14 or newer.Not yet. The iOS app is building now and coming soon. Until then, CoRoute runs on Android 6.0 or newer.

Does it work without mobile data?

Partly. Saved maps and crash detection work without signal. Live positions and talk need data; your route is sent later and an SOS keeps retrying. Agree on a regroup point before long dead stretches.

What do the numbers on this site say about me?

Nothing. They are rounded totals, shown only with at least ten riders or rides behind them. Nothing per city, route or rider. No cookies, no trackers.

How are the rider stories chosen?

Only riders who allow it after a trip can appear. Our team reads every story and checks every photo first. We never add our own.

Does Coroute replace calling for help?

No. In an emergency in India, call 112 first. Coroute tells your group, guardians and, if you allow it, nearby riders. It cannot promise anyone will come. See how safety works.

Ready for the next long ride?

Android 6.0 or newer. Free to start.
