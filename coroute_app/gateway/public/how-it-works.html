How it works

Install. Join. Ride. Look back.

Four steps from the download to the trip report. Every rider needs the Android app.

Get the app

The four steps

  1. Install and sign up

    Download the APK, sign up with your phone number or Google, add your bike, an emergency contact and your medical ID.

  2. Create or join

    One rider creates the ride, sets the destination and stops, and shares the code. Others join with the code or the link.

  3. Ride

    Start the ride. The map, ahead and behind, the intercom and the crash alarm keep running with the phone in your pocket.

  4. Look back

    End the ride. It lands in your timeline with the route, stops and events, and family links stop working.

Questions

Short answers.

Is Coroute free?

Community Rider is free with no ads. CoRoute Pro, Road Captain Pass and Tour Operator are paid plans, chosen and paid for by UPI inside the app.

How many riders can join one ride?

Up to __MAX_RIDERS__ riders in one ride group.

Does every rider need the app?

Yes. Each rider needs Coroute on an Android phone, an account, and the ride code or invite link. Family can follow a live share link in a browser without the app.

Is there an iPhone version?

Coroute is Android only. No iOS version yet.

Does it work without mobile data?

Positions and the intercom need data. When the signal drops, your route is kept on the phone and sent later, an SOS waits and retries, the emergency SMS option sends a text from your SIM, and the offline mesh lets phones relay to each other.

Does Coroute replace calling for help?

No. In an emergency in India, call 112 first. Coroute tells your group, your guardians and, if you allow it, nearby riders. It cannot promise that anyone will come. See how safety works.
