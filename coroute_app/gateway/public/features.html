Safety

Help that reaches your people, even off the grid.

Every safety feature is free for every rider, solo or in a group.

  • Crash detection

    A hard fall starts a 30-second check. No answer, and help is asked for.

  • Hold-to-send SOS

    Hold for three seconds. Your group, emergency contacts and guardians are told.

  • SOS without signal

    SMS from your phone, phone to phone over a shared hotspot, then server SMS to your contacts.

  • Siren alerts

    Ambulance, police and fire sirens announced in your earbuds, with a cue to give way.

  • Guardians at home

    Family follow a private link in any browser and get a message when you arrive.

  • Medical ID

    Blood group, allergies and a contact, shown only while an emergency is open.

  • Rider-down and missing-rider checks

    A fallen bike or a rider gone quiet gets an "Are you OK?" before anyone worries.

  • Speed limits and road hazards

    Live limits, sharp-bend warnings and hazards the lead marks, saved for later rides.

  • Offline maps and fuel range

    Save the route map on Wi-Fi, and know your range for the bike you ride.

Group riding

Nobody quietly dropped, from the first morning to the last.

One live map for the whole group, with roles that keep the formation together.

  • Live group map

    Every rider in road order, with a 3D marker for each bike.

  • Lead, middle and sweeper

    The lead and sweeper hear when someone falls behind or the group stretches.

  • Regroup points

    One tap calls a short stop; smart regroup points hold the plan without signal.

  • Routes and stops, planned once

    Spoken route guide with re-routing back to the group's plan.

  • Pillion and bike swaps

    Seat a pillion on your bike, or swap bikes mid-trip, and the map follows.

  • Public ride, if you want it

    Riders nearby see a rough spot and group size, never names. Off unless the host turns it on.

Talk

A live intercom in every helmet, no extra hardware.

Talk while you ride, through the headset you already have or the phone.

  • Push-to-talk

    Hold to talk to everyone, or to one rider.

  • Hands-free

    Just speak; a clear on-air sign shows when you are heard.

  • Clean voice

    Wind, engine rumble and breath are cleaned out before your group hears you.

  • Helmet sets and earbuds

    Works with your Bluetooth helmet set; alerts lower your music, then bring it back.

  • Talk from the lock screen

    Talk and Mute buttons on the ride notification, without unlocking.

  • Never recorded

    Voice is relayed live to your group only. Nothing is stored.

Community

Your crew, your friends, your next ride.

Crews, polls and friends keep the group going between rides.

  • Friends

    Add riders by their unique CoRoute username.

  • Crews

    Your regular riding group, with members and the next ride in one place.

  • Polls

    Vote on where to ride next, right inside the crew.

  • Crew ride invites

    Invite the whole crew to a ride with one push notification.

  • Leaderboards

    Top riders by score, distance and hours, by username. Hide yours any time.

  • Rider stories

    Rate the trip and share the story. Approved stories appear on this site.

Trips and reports

Every ride becomes a story you keep.

A full report for the group the moment the ride ends.

  • Trip report

    Distance, moving time, stops and every rider's route on one map.

  • Route replay

    Watch the group's ride play back, rider by rider.

  • Rider score

    A score for smooth braking and steady speed, to ride better each time.

  • Share card and GPX

    One image for the group chat, and the route as a GPX file.

  • Private safety report

    Hard braking, alerts and regroups from your ride, for you only.

  • Your ride analytics

    Kilometres, hours and averages over time, private to you.

Clubs and operators

  • Big groups on one map

    Every rider on the same map, intercom and safety net, with your ride captains as lead and sweeper.

  • Safety your riders can see

    Crash detection, SOS and guardian links for every rider who joins your ride.

    Tell us about your club or company and the rides you run. We reply by e-mail.
