Features

Built around the ride.

Coroute is organised the way a ride is. What you set up before you leave, what helps on the road, and what stays after you park.

Before the ride

Set it up once. Everyone joins the same plan.

Pre-ride checklist

Before each ride: location and notification permissions, battery, your emergency contact and a short kit list for fuel, gear and papers. It stays on your phone.

Create a group

The lead names the ride and gets a six-digit ride code and an invite link. Up to __MAX_RIDERS__ riders can join one group.

Destination

Set where the ride ends. Everyone sees the route, the distance left and an arrival time.

Stops

Plan fuel, food, rest and meeting point stops on the route. Every rider sees them on the map.

Emergency contact

Needed before you ride in a group. Only the members of your ride group can see it, so they can reach the right person.

Medical info Optional

Blood group, allergies and short notes, only if you fill them in. Your group sees them only while your SOS or crash alert is open.

Route and stops

Plan the stops before anyone has to ask.

Add stops along the route: Fuel, Food, Rest, Meeting point, or a plain Stop. Each one shows on every rider's map, with the destination at the end.

The lead can also long-press the map during the ride to set a meeting point or add a stop right there.

02 of 03

During the ride

See the group, talk to it, and keep it together. Built for a phone on the handlebar and gloves on your hands.

Live group map

Know where the group is without searching for it.

Every rider is on one map with their position, speed and heading. You see how far the nearest riders are ahead of you and behind you, and how spread out the whole group is.

The road you have already ridden fades, so the map shows what is left.

  • The group can tell "no signal" from "app closed".
  • Maps from OpenStreetMap.

See the group

Live map

Every rider on one map, with the planned route, the stops and the destination.

Rider positions and heading

Position, speed and heading for each rider, updated while you move.

Distance ahead and behind

How far the nearest riders are in front of you and behind you, along the road.

Group spread

The distance between the first and the last rider, so the lead knows when to ease off.

Talk

Group intercom

Hold to talk to everyone. Hands-free mode sends only while you speak. Voice is relayed live and never recorded.

Private talk

Pick one rider in "Talk to" and speak only to them. The rest of the group does not hear it.

Quick replies

One tap sends Fuel stop, Wait for me, All good, Slow down, Taking a break, Bike problem or Road hazard ahead. Large buttons that work with gloves.

Spoken alerts

Emergency alerts and warnings are read out by your phone's own text-to-speech. Nothing is sent anywhere. You can switch them off.

Stops and arrivals

Regroup without asking where everyone is.

Each rider is marked as they reach a planned stop. The stop is done when the whole group is there. Riding past a stop is noted too.

Arrival time and how long each rider stayed go into the ride timeline.

Auto reroute

Leave the route and Coroute works out a new one from where you are.

Speed limit awareness

The lead can set a speed limit for the group. Your speed turns red above it. Riding over it for more than a few seconds is logged, and the group is told once, not every time.

Meet here

The lead long-presses a place on the map and sets it as the group's meeting point.

Share ETA

Send a short message with where you are going, the distance left and when you expect to arrive. Share it through any messaging app.

SOS

Hold to send. It reaches the whole group with your position and stays on screen until it is resolved. How safety works.

Large ride notification

The ride, on your lock screen.

During a ride the notification shows your destination, the nearest riders ahead and behind with their distance, and quick actions: SOS (hold to send), Wait for me and Open map.

The lock screen view is your choice. Turn it off and the lock screen shows only that a ride is active. Phone numbers are never shown there.

Ride light

Data saver

One switch for prepaid plans and weak signal. The intercom uses half the data and your position is sent every 5 seconds instead of every 2.5. Riders with and without it hear each other normally.

Day and night

A light theme for bright sun and a dark one for night riding. It switches by itself at sunrise and sunset where you are. Worked out on your phone.

Large ride notification

Riders ahead and behind, and quick actions, on the home and lock screen. You can switch it off.

Easy on the battery

GPS slows down when you stop, and audio is sent only while someone talks. Without signal your route is kept on the phone and sent later.

03 of 03

After the ride

The ride does not disappear when the engine stops.

Trip report

Every rider's route, on one map.

Ending the ride saves it for everyone. The trip report shows each rider's route in their own colour, where they started, waited and finished, the distance and the riding time.

Replay the whole ride, or save your own route as a GPX file.

Ride history

Every ride you were part of, in one list. Summaries stay until you delete them.

Trip report

Distance, riding time, stops and top speed for each rider, with every route in its own colour.

Group timeline

One shared list of what happened: who joined, stopped and for how long, fell behind, lost signal, went off the route or arrived.

Route replay

Play the whole ride back and watch the group move along the road.

Ride summary and share card

A short summary of the ride, and a picture card of it you can share wherever you like.

GPX export

Save your own route as a GPX file for other map apps.

Recorded routes are deleted after 90 days. Your account and ride summaries stay until you delete them. Privacy policy.
