Features

Built around the ride.

Up to __MAX_RIDERS__ riders can join one group. Here is what the app does before, during and after.

Get the app

Before the ride Plan once, ride light

  • Pre-ride checklist

    Permissions, battery and contacts checked before you roll.

  • Create a group

    One ride code or link. Riders join from any Android phone.

  • Destination and stops

    Fuel, food and rest stops on the route before anyone asks.

  • Fuel profile

    Tank size and mileage give the group a usable range.

  • Sweeper role

    Assign the rider who stays last and closes the group.

  • Emergency contact and medical ID

    Who to tell and what a responder should know.

During the ride Phone in the pocket

  • Live convoy map

    Every rider on one map, with your route and the stops.

  • Ahead or behind

    GPS telemetry ranks the group by road distance from you.

  • Lock-screen radar

    Riders ahead and behind in the ride notification.

  • Push-to-talk intercom

    Hold to talk to the group or to one rider. Not recorded.

  • Offline P2P mesh

    Phones relay positions to each other where there is no signal.

  • Quick status cards

    Slow down, stop ahead, all good: one tap, no typing.

  • Road hazards

    Mark a pothole or oil and the riders behind are warned.

  • Fuel and essentials

    Fuel, water, repairs and hospitals along the next stretch.

  • Regroup

    When the group spreads out, the leader is told.

  • Crash detection

    A hard fall starts a countdown, then the alarm.

  • Hold-to-send SOS

    Your group gets your position and a route to you.

  • Emergency SMS

    No internet? The SOS goes as a text from your SIM.

  • Nearby rider assist

    Riders of other Coroute groups on your road can offer help.

  • Guardian alerts

    Family is told on a crash, an SOS, or a long stop.

After the ride Yours to keep

  • Ride timeline

    Every ride, in order: route, stops, alerts and who rode.

  • Trips and replay

    Distance, time and the route replayed on the map.

  • Live share links

    Family follows a ride in the browser while it is on.

  • Your account

    Phone or Google sign-in. Data held in Oracle Cloud.

  • Plans in the app

    Choose a plan in the app and pay by UPI through Razorpay.

  • Private by default

    GPS traces are removed after a ride ends. Voice is never stored.

Free to start.

Community Rider costs nothing. Paid plans, chosen in the app, add the mesh, larger convoys and more.
