Built around the long ride.

Before the ride Plan once, ride light

  • Destination and stops

    Fuel, food and rest stops set on the route before anyone rolls off, so the whole group rides the same plan.

  • Maps saved for the route

    Save the map along your planned route on Wi-Fi. It stays on your phone, so it still shows where there is no signal.

  • Fuel profile

    Tank size and mileage stay on your phone. CoRoute works out your range and reminds you to fill up in time.

  • Lead and sweeper

    The lead picks an experienced rider to stay last and close the formation, so nobody is left behind on a climb.

  • Pre-ride checklist

    Permissions, battery and emergency contact checked before your wheels turn, not discovered missing on the highway.

  • Emergency contact and medical ID

    Blood group, allergies and a contact number, ready for first responders if they are ever needed.

During the ride Together on the road, looked after

  • Live convoy map

    Every rider on one shared map, updated every few seconds, with who is ahead and who is behind by road distance.

  • Push-to-talk intercom

    Hold to talk to the group or to one rider, through your helmet headset or the phone. Voice is relayed live and never recorded.

  • Microphone off by default

    Nothing is heard until you hold Talk, or switch on hands-free mode yourself, which shows a clear on-air sign.

  • Crash detection

    Runs on your phone through the ride. A hard fall starts a 15 second alarm you can cancel before anyone is told.

  • Hold-to-send SOS

    Your group hears an alarm and gets a route to you, guardians are notified, and without internet a ready-to-send text with your location opens.

  • Guardian live links

    Family follow the ride in any browser, at the level you choose. They are told about a crash, an SOS or a long unexpected stop.

  • Fuel range in the group

    Share just your remaining range, and find fuel, puncture repair, food and medical help within reach of the road.

  • Break reminders

    After about two hours of riding without a proper stop, a gentle reminder to take ten minutes off the bike.

  • Nobody quietly dropped

    The lead and the sweeper are told when a rider falls behind the sweeper, stops sending updates or runs low on battery.

  • Glove-friendly ride screen

    Big controls for a handlebar mount, readable in direct sun, with the screen kept awake while you ride.

  • Short gaps are fine

    When the signal drops your route is kept on the phone and sent later, and an SOS keeps retrying until it gets through.

  • Settings changed mid-ride?

    If Android switches off a permission the ride needs, a clear banner says so and opens the right settings screen in one tap.

After the ride A story to keep, private by design

  • Trip report

    Distance, moving time, stops and every rider's route on one map, with the ride's timeline beneath it.

  • Route replay

    Watch the group's ride play back on the map, rider by rider, from the first morning to the last stop.

  • Share card and GPX

    One image for the group chat, and the route as a GPX file for your own archive or another app.

  • Rider stories

    Rate the trip and tell the story. If you allow it and the CoRoute team approves it, it appears in rider reviews.

  • Links that end

    Guardian links stop when the ride ends. Emergency links stop after 30 minutes or when the alert is closed.

  • Private by default

    No ads, no trackers, no sale of data. Recorded routes are deleted after 90 days, and you can delete your account in the app.
