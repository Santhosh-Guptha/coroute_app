Safety

Look out for each other.

What Coroute does when something goes wrong on a ride, what it shares and with whom, and what it cannot do.

Coroute does not replace emergency services.

In an emergency in India, call 112 first. Coroute helps your group and nearby riders know and respond. It cannot promise that anyone will.

SOS

Hold to send.

Hold the SOS button to send it, so a tap in a pocket does not raise an alarm. The alert reaches your whole group with your position. It stays on every screen until it is resolved.

Pick a reason if you can: medical, breakdown, crash, or your own words. From the same screen you can call or text your emergency contact.

You can also start SOS from the ride notification on the lock screen. Nothing is sent until you hold the button.

Quick access to Indian Emergency Services

In the emergency sheet and the assist sheet, dedicated quick-dial buttons let you reach official emergency response services immediately:

  • 112 National Emergency: Direct dial to India's unified emergency helpline.
  • 108 Ambulance: One-tap access to emergency medical and ambulance dispatch.
  • 100 Police: Quick connection to local police assistance.

Report a rider down on the route

If you see a motorcyclist down on the road, do not wait for them to reach their phone. Any rider in the convoy can long-press the map to report a rider down. The report immediately alerts your whole group and nearby riders on the Safety Network with the exact position and direction.

  • No signal? The SOS or rider down report is kept on your phone and sent once, as soon as the connection returns.

Convoy cohesionNew in 3.16

Nobody left behind.

A designated sweeper at the tail and live radar on your lock screen keep the group together without constant phone checks.

Designated sweeper role

The ride lead can assign a trusted rider as the sweeper at the tail of the convoy. The app continuously tracks the distance between each rider and the sweeper along the planned route.

  • Behind the sweeper: If a rider falls more than 500 metres behind the sweeper for 60 seconds, both the lead and sweeper hear an alert.
  • Stale updates: If a rider stops updating or loses signal on a remote stretch, the tail is alerted to check on them.
  • Low-battery alerts: The lead and sweeper hear when any rider's battery falls to 15%, before their phone shuts down.

Smart Ride Lock-Screen Notification

Glance at your convoy status from a handlebar mount without unlocking the screen or wearing down your battery.

  • Convoy radar: See who is riding ahead and behind with live road distances at a glance.
  • Next fuel distance: Shows road kilometres to the next upcoming fuel stop on your route.
  • Battery-efficient telemetry: Adjusts background update cadence based on speed and battery level, conserving power on long rides while pushing emergencies instantly.
  • One-tap actions: Hold SOS over the lock screen, tap Wait for me, or open the full map with gloved hands.

Automatic crash detectionNew in 3.15

If you cannot press SOS.

During a ride, above 25 km/h, your phone reads its motion sensor to notice a crash. This happens on your phone only. Motion data is not sent anywhere and not stored.

  1. After a hard impact and a sudden stop, an alarm sounds: "Possible accident detected. Are you okay?"
  2. A 15 second countdown starts. The alarm can show over the lock screen, so you can answer without unlocking.
  3. Tap I'm OK and nothing is sent.
  4. Tap Need Help, or do not answer, and an SOS marked as automatic goes to your group. It carries your position, direction, speed before the impact and the time.

Our server also watches for a rider who stops suddenly from high speed and does not move for 2 minutes, or loses signal right after riding fast. It then asks the lead and the nearest riders to check on that rider.

What crash detection cannot do

  • It runs only during a ride and above 25 km/h. Slow falls and low speed crashes are not detected.
  • A phone that is thrown far, broken or out of battery cannot raise the alarm.
  • It can go off when nothing happened, for example if the phone falls hard. Tap I'm OK.
  • On some phones, Do Not Disturb may keep the alarm quiet.
  • You can switch crash detection off in the app at any time.

Emergency textsNew in 3.15

When there is no internet.

This is off until you switch it on. When it is on and your SOS cannot reach our server, your own phone sends a text message (SMS) with a map link to your position.

The texts go from your phone and your SIM, not from us. Your carrier may charge for them.

To make this work, your phone keeps the numbers of the other riders in your group during the ride. They are stored encrypted, never shown on screen, and deleted when the ride ends.

Do not want to receive these texts? Switch off "Receive emergency texts from my ride group" in your profile.

Who gets the text

  • Your emergency contact
  • Your lead
  • The nearest riders in your group

At most 10 people. Only when the SOS could not be sent over the internet.

Route EssentialsNew in 3.17

Safety along your corridor.

When a tyre punctures or someone needs medical attention on a highway, knowing what lies ahead on your exact road matters far more than straight-line pins on a generic map.

Route Essentials scans a safety corridor within 1.5 km of your planned route for vital facilities, calculating actual road access and return distance rather than straight-line estimates.

  • Medical and hospitals: Discovers hospitals and emergency medical facilities along the road corridor, showing detour distances and one-tap navigation.
  • Punctures and mechanics: Finds motorcycle repair shops and tyre puncture repair points on your route so minor mechanical issues do not strand you.
  • Fuel corridor discovery: Maps verified fuel stations within 1.5 km of your route, checking highway access and detour kilometres.
  • Cached on your device: Discovered essentials stay cached on your phone, remaining accessible even when cellular data drops along remote roads.

Any rider can suggest or add a discovered safety stop directly to the group itinerary.

Smart Fuel ProfileNew in 3.17

Never run dry on remote stretches.

Motorcycle fuel gauges can be unreliable, and remote Indian highways often have long gaps between open petrol pumps. Coroute helps your group plan fuel stops well before reserve.

  • Tank range and reserve buffer: Configure your full tank range and reserve. Coroute adds a safety buffer (default 20 km) so you never budget fuel down to an empty tank.
  • 80% range reminder: When 80% of your usable fuel range is covered, the app sounds a timely reminder to find fuel before you touch your reserve buffer.
  • Opt-in group fuel estimation: Riders can choose to share their remaining usable range with the group. The lead sees who has the shortest range and can schedule stops that keep everyone moving safely.
  • Private by design: Your tank size, mileage and litres never leave your device. Only the calculated usable kilometres are shared with the active ride group, and sharing stops immediately when you opt out or end the ride.

Ride GuardianNew in 3.17

Keep family close without an app.

Let family and loved ones follow your ride from any web browser on phone or desktop. No Coroute account, login, or app installation is required.

Every Ride Guardian link provides strong privacy protections and clear control:

  • Optional PIN protection: Secure your tracking link with an optional 4 to 8 digit PIN that viewers must enter to view your ride.
  • Three privacy tiers: Select the exact degree of visibility you want to share:

1. Basic tier: Shows ride milestones (started, paused, resumed, ended), planned stop arrivals, and overall group rider count without sharing live GPS coordinates.

2. Live tier: Displays real-time location updates, progress along the route and timeline updates while the ride is active.

3. Emergency tier: Keeps your position completely private during normal riding, sharing location and emergency details only if an SOS or crash alarm is actively open.

  • Automatic expiry: Links expire automatically 6 hours after the ride ends, or after a chosen period of up to 14 days.
  • Instant pause or revoke: Pause or revoke any link at any moment in the app. When hidden or revoked, the browser view immediately removes private location data.

Coroute Safety NetworkNew in 3.15

What if another rider can reach them first?

The closest rider may be the first person able to help. Sometimes that rider is in another group.

When you raise an SOS or a crash alert, your own group is always alerted first. Coroute also looks for a few riders of other groups who are already riding toward you on the same road, and who may reach you sooner.

At most 3 riders are asked at a time, never everyone nearby. If one says no, the next may be asked.

Before they agree, they see only

  • The place of the emergency
  • How far it is, and roughly how long it takes to get there

Not your name, your group, your phone number, your ride mates or anyone's position.

If they tap "I Can Help"

They also see your first name and your vehicle type and colour, so they can find you. Your medical info only if you switched that on. Phone numbers are never given to riders of other groups.

Your group sees that a nearby rider is coming, their first name, how far away they are and where they are on the way. Not their group.

Only where Coroute groups ride

Nearby help works only when other Coroute groups are riding on the same road at that moment. On many roads there will be nobody. Do not count on it.

You can opt out

Switch off "Help nearby riders" and you are never asked. Switch off "Ask nearby riders to help me" and other groups are never asked for you. Your own group still is.

Separate from Public or Private

Nearby help works the same whether your group is Public or Private. Safety settings are separate from social visibility.

Accident warningsNew in 3.15

One emergency should not become another.

Riders of other groups who are heading toward an accident on the same road get a short caution, with the place only and no names. The phone can read it out as they get closer.

A warning is a reason to slow down and look. It does not mean the road is closed or that help has arrived.

The link works for 30 minutes, or until the rider's group closes the alert or the link is revoked in the app. When an SOS or crash alert opens, the incident sheet can also show the nearest hospital from OpenStreetMap, with its distance and a Navigate button. The alert never waits for that lookup, and in some places no hospital is listed.

  • Route weather forecast. Open-Meteo forecasts rain and thunderstorms along your planned road corridor based on projected arrival times. One clear summary at trip review and ride start (such as "Rain likely after Warangal around 3 PM"), with automated warnings for impending rain. Disabled in data saver mode.
  • Route Essentials discovery. Scans a 1.5 km corridor along the route for hospitals, motorcycle mechanics, tyre puncture repair and fuel stations with road access distances.
  • Smart Fuel safety buffer. Tank range with 80% reminder, reserve buffer, and opt-in group fuel estimation so the convoy avoids running dry on remote stretches.
  • Ride Guardian links. Secure browser tracking for family with optional PIN protection and 3 privacy tiers (Basic, Live, Emergency).
  • Lock-screen convoy radar. Ongoing notification with Ahead and Behind radar, next fuel distance, battery-efficient telemetry, and low-battery alerts.

False alarms

Mistakes happen. Say so.

If an alarm went off by mistake, tap I'm OK. Your group sees that you said you are OK.

  • Cancel before it is sent. During the 15 second crash countdown, I'm OK sends nothing. Letting go of SOS before it fills sends nothing.
  • Cancel a sent SOS. It is closed as a false alarm, and your group sees that it was one.
  • Repeated false alarms limit outside requests. Coroute keeps the times of your last 10 false alarms that other groups were asked to help with. An account with many gets fewer requests sent to other groups.
  • Never your own group. Your own group is always alerted, whatever your history.

Coroute complements emergency services. It does not replace them.

In an emergency in India, call 112. Elsewhere, call your local emergency number.

Coroute depends on phones, batteries, GPS, mobile networks, and on other people being nearby and able to help. Any of these can fail. No response is guaranteed, and no alert is a substitute for professional help.
