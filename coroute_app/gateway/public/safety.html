Safety

Look out for each other.

Coroute helps your group and nearby riders know when something is wrong. It does not replace emergency services.

In an emergency in India, call 112 first.

Automatic crash detectionNew in 3.15

What happens on a crash.

If the phone detects a hard fall, a countdown starts. If you do not cancel, your group gets an alarm with your position, your guardians get a notification, and, if you switched it on, an emergency text goes from your SIM. Riders of other Coroute groups on the same road may be asked to help.

  1. The phone notices

    The motion sensor reads a hard impact during a ride. A full-screen countdown opens, even over the lock screen.

  2. You can cancel

    Dropped the phone? Tap cancel. Nothing is sent and nobody is told.

  3. The alarm goes out

    Your group hears it and sees a route to you. Guardians get a notification and a live link. The SMS goes if you enabled it.

  4. Someone resolves it

    You or a rider who reached you marks it resolved. Everyone who was told sees that too.

Hold-to-send SOS

Who gets told.

The same people are told whether the alarm is automatic or you hold the SOS button yourself.

  • Your group

    An alarm, your position, and a route to you by road.

  • Your guardians

    Family you added get a push notification and a live link.

  • Your emergency contact, by SMS

    If you switched it on, a text with your position from your own SIM.

  • Nearby riders

    Riders of other Coroute groups already on your road, if you allow it.

Coroute Safety NetworkNew in 3.15

What if another rider can reach you first?

Nearby help works only when other Coroute groups are riding on the same road at that moment. On many roads there will be nobody. Do not count on it.

Guardians and live links

Keep family close without an app.

Add guardians in the app. They follow the ride in a browser through a link you share, and are notified on a crash, an SOS, a long unexpected stop, or when you go offline for a while. Links stop working when the ride ends.

Live link and nearest hospitalNew in 3.16

Help from outside the app.

During an open SOS, the rider or the lead can share a live emergency link with anyone, for example family or the emergency services. It opens a plain web page with the rider's first name, last position, the time of that position and a Call 112 button. Nothing else. When an SOS or crash alert opens, the incident sheet can also show the nearest hospital from OpenStreetMap, with its distance and a Navigate button.

False alarms

Mistakes happen. Say so.

Cancel during the countdown and nothing goes out. If an alarm did go out, mark it resolved and everyone who was told sees that. Speed warnings and accident warnings from other groups are advisory only.

Coroute complements emergency services. It does not replace them. In an emergency in India, call 112. Elsewhere, call your local emergency number. Coroute cannot promise that anyone will respond.
