Crash detection

What happens on a crash.

Runs on your phone, even when locked. Motion data never leaves it.

  1. A hard fall is noticed

    Bumps and braking do not count

  2. 15 seconds to cancel

    Loud alarm over the lock screen

  3. Your group is alerted

    An alarm and a route to you

  4. Guardians are told

    They follow on their link

  5. A text, if offline

    Sent from your own SIM

  6. Hospital, then resolved

    Everyone sees it is handled

False alarms

Mistakes happen. Say so.

Cancel in time and nothing goes out. Sent by mistake? Mark it resolved.

Coroute complements emergency services. It does not replace them. In an emergency in India, call 112. Elsewhere, call your local emergency number. Coroute cannot promise that anyone will respond.
